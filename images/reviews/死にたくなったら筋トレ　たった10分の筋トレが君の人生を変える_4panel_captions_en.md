He wears a robe that drapes over his well-trained physique, symbolizing the balance of strength and intellect, and gestures silently towards a diagram of muscles and the heart.

Her expression is determined, and beside her lie an open abacus and the Dutch book. Sweat flies from her, resembling ink splashes that echo the strokes of sumi-e art. The window reveals the dawn breaking, symbolizing a fresh start.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Kneeling by the window, the townsgirl writes a waka with peaceful eyes and a faint smile. The poem is elegantly displayed on a vertical tanzaku, showcasing graceful brushwork.  
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "If I do not move, my heart will freeze; this body must be trained to know the strength of living."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「動かねば  
　心も凍る  
　この身こそ  
　鍛へて知るや  
　生くる力を」
