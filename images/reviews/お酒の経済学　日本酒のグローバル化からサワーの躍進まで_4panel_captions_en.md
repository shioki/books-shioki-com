### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through a bustling Edo street, surrounded by sake shops. She overhears merchants expressing their concern about the decline in sake consumption, glancing thoughtfully at the weathered sake barrels. The background features traditional wooden storefronts and lanterns adorned with sake brewery symbols.
**Dialogue**: "People drink less these days..."

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her cozy study, filled with scrolls and Dutch literature, the townsgirl discovers a new treatise by Tsuru Yasushi. The imagined scholar, depicted with a calm demeanor and wise eyes, is portrayed on a hanging scroll. She is captivated by the diagrams illustrating trade routes and sake export trends.
**Dialogue**: [None]

### Panel 3
**Scene**: Motivated by her studies, she experiments in a sake brewery courtyard, organizing bottles labeled “生酛,” “木桶,” and “輸出.” She sketches a chart on a wooden board that indicates a rise in foreign demand. Nearby, foreign visitors dressed in Edo attire enjoy her sake, and her face beams with excitement as she realizes the global potential of tradition.
**Dialogue**: [None]

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As dusk falls, she sits beside a softly glowing paper lantern, brush in hand, composing a waka on a tanzaku. Her expression is calm yet filled with hope, as the wind gently stirs the sake barrels and the river sparkles faintly in the background.
**Dialogue**: [None]
**Tanka (translation)**: "The ancient flavor crosses the sea, the path of sake. Culture blends together, creating a new fragrance."
