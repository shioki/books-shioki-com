### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through an Edo street at dawn, appearing tired and sighing as she carries scrolls and an abacus. She reflects on her dwindling energy despite her diligent studies, with wooden storefronts and morning vendors bustling in the background.
**Dialogue**: "Why do I feel so drained even though I study so hard?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a small temple school, the townsgirl opens a Dutch text alongside a mysterious scroll that illustrates “scientific methods of cheer.” A learned man resembling Hideo Hotta appears, calm and intellectual, pointing to a diagram that states “action shapes emotion.”
**Dialogue**: "You see, action shapes emotion."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: Inspired, the townsgirl stands confidently, forcing a bright smile as she sweeps the street with enthusiasm. Her neighbors, taken aback by her cheerfulness, begin to smile as well. The brushwork conveys motion and humor, with a sparrow perched on her broom.
**Dialogue**: "Look at me go!"

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At sunset, she kneels by the riverbank, writing a tanka on a small scroll. Her expression is soft and content, with the wind gently moving her hair. The short poem appears in Japanese beside her brush.
**Tanka (translation)**: "With a single smile, my body calls my heart; action is the source of my energy."
**Tanka (original)**: 「笑みひとつ  
　身体が先に  
　心呼ぶ  
　行動こそが  
　元気のもとに」
