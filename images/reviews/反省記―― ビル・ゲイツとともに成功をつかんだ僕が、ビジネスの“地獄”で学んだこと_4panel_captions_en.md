### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through a lively Edo street at dusk, pausing to observe a merchant arguing with his apprentice over a failed invention. She tilts her head thoughtfully, sensing the connection between success and failure, as warm paper lantern light flickers over her curious face.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a serene temple study, the townsgirl studies a Dutch science book and a mysterious foreign scroll with the name “西 和彦.” The ink portrait on the scroll depicts a calm, thoughtful man with round spectacles, who seems to gaze back at her. She imagines him as a teacher whispering about the essence of creation.
**Dialogue**: “True creation begins with wonder and gratitude.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: Inspired, she constructs a small wooden calculating device by candlelight, merging abacus logic with gears she carved herself. When the device collapses, she laughs softly and refocuses her efforts. Her friend peeks in, surprised by her calm determination.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Dawn light filters through paper shoji screens. The device ticks gently beside her ink brush as she writes a tanka on a hanging scroll, her expression serene, embodying the book’s themes of reflection and renewal.  
**Tanka (translation)**: Even lost days harbor light within the heart; weaving tomorrow with threads of gratitude.  
**Tanka (original)**: 失（う）せし日も　光を宿す　心かな  
感謝の糸に　未来（あす）を紡ぎぬ
