### Panel 3

**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As night descends, the townsgirl sits beside a lantern, brush poised in her hand, gazing at the stars reflected in her inkstone. She writes a tanka on a hanging scroll, the characters flowing gracefully in vertical calligraphy. Her expression is calm yet contemplative, capturing the essence of the book's message about ethical imagination.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "Inquiring voices ask whom to envision for the unseen future, as I let my brush glide, contemplating humanity."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「見ぬ未来　誰を描けと　問ふ声に　筆すべらせて　人を想へり」
