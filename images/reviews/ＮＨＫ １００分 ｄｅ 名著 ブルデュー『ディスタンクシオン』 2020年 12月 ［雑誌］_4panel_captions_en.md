### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through an Edo street, observing people admiring paintings and tea sets. She tilts her head in curiosity, pondering why certain tastes are deemed "refined." The background features a merchant’s shop and townsfolk engaged in conversation about "good taste." Her expression reflects a blend of curiosity and quiet doubt.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In a dimly lit study filled with scrolls and Dutch books, the townsgirl reads a mysterious imported treatise. Next to her stands a calm, scholarly figure resembling a broadcaster, an older man in simple robes, holding a brush and gently lecturing. He gestures toward a diagram of layered circles labeled "Habitus" and "Cultural Capital." The townsgirl listens intently, fascinated by the discussion.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl participates in a tea gathering. When the guests commend a particular tea for its elegance, she cheerfully presents a different blend from a common merchant, explaining its unique beauty. The guests pause in surprise, realizing their preconceived notions. The dynamic brushstrokes convey her confidence and humor.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At night, the townsgirl sits by lamplight, gazing at her reflection in a water basin. She holds a brush and writes a waka poem on a vertical tanzaku. The poem expresses a serene sentiment, symbolizing "hope without illusion."
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: What is preference?  
From the shadow of the shrine,  
A flower blooms—  
A dream without illusion,  
Igniting hope within.  
**Tanka (original)**: 好みとは  
社のかげより  
咲く花か  
幻なき夢  
希みを灯す
