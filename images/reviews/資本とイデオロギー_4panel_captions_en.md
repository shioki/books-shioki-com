### Panel 1
**Scene**: In a bustling Edo street filled with merchants and scholars, the townsgirl pauses, troubled by a wealthy merchant's boast about the natural order of wealth and power. She gazes at the market where beggars and nobles coexist, a gentle breeze lifting her sleeve as she questions the nature of inequality.  
**Dialogue**: "Is inequality truly 'natural'?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a dimly lit terakoya, the townsgirl studies a Dutch text and scrolls, while a portrait-like illustration of Thomas Piketty hangs on the wall. Yamagata Hiroo, an elder with glasses, explains concepts with gestures, while Morimoto Masashi, a thoughtful scribe, observes quietly. The townsgirl's eyes widen as she realizes that inequality is shaped by ideologies rather than nature.  
**Dialogue**: "I see now, it's not nature, but ideology that creates inequality."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: At a town meeting illuminated by lantern light, the townsgirl passionately draws charts on a wooden board, illustrating how taxes and ownership can transform society. The townsfolk react with surprise and laughter as she asserts that they can choose a different path. Ink splashes dynamically around her, capturing her conviction and humor as a child exclaims, "So we can rebuild our world!"  
**Dialogue**: "Even we can choose another way!"

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Under the night sky, the townsgirl sits alone, writing a tanka on a tanzaku strip with determination. The moonlight glimmers on her ink as she recites her poem softly.  
**Tanka (translation)**: "Inequality is the shape of shadows chosen by people; with the light of wisdom, we illuminate the world."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「  
格差とは  
人の選びし  
影のかたち  
知恵の灯もて  
世を照らすかな  
」
