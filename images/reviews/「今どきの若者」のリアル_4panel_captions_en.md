### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through a lively Edo street, listening to older townspeople grumbling about the younger generation. She pauses, tilting her head in curiosity while holding a bamboo writing tablet. The background features wooden shop signs and a small teahouse, highlighting the contrast between generations. Her eyes show confusion mixed with keen interest, sensing a deeper social divide behind their casual remarks.
**Dialogue**: “Young folks these days…”

### Panel 2
**Scene**: At her candle-lit desk, the townsgirl studies a Dutch-bound book titled “On the Nature of Youth.” A portrait scroll of a scholarly man resembling the author hangs nearby, depicted as a calm, middle-aged scholar in Edo-style academic robes. His serene expression seems to advise her on the importance of understanding the structure that shapes youth. She nods, brush poised mid-air, absorbing the wisdom.
**Dialogue**: “Look not at youth alone, but at the structure that shapes them.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: She participates in a lively discussion with young apprentices and elders at a riverside evening gathering. Using abacus beads and clever metaphors, she explains how the fortunes of different generations depend on the same shared “ledger of society.” The group laughs in surprise at her cleverness as she transforms abstract inequality into a humorous math riddle. The moonlight glimmers on the river, symbolizing reflection and connection.
**Dialogue**: “Our fortunes are all in the same ledger!”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Alone under the night sky, the townsgirl kneels by her paper lantern, composing a waka filled with gentle melancholy yet hope. Her brush moves gracefully across the paper, with the short poem clearly visible on a tanzaku slip beside her. The ink strokes faintly glow in the moonlight.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]
**Tanka (translation)**: The young voices reflect the mirror of society, swaying in the wind. When we adjust our stance, the future becomes clear.
**Tanka (original)**: 若き声　映すは世の鏡　揺らぐ風  
　構え直せば　未来透け見ゆ
