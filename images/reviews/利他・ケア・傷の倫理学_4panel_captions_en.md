### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl assists an elderly vendor in picking up fallen apples at the marketplace. A bystander scolds her for wasting time, but she responds with a gentle smile. The scene captures the moment in motion — apples rolling and her sleeves fluttering — illustrating her quiet understanding that care is about "continuing the dialogue," not about being right. A humorous detail shows a stray cat also pawing at an apple.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: In the evening, the townsgirl sits by the window with an ink brush poised, composing a waka on a narrow strip of paper. A soft breeze stirs the candle flame, and her expression is calm and reflective.
**Dialogue**: None
**Tanka (translation)**: Hesitating yet still extending my warm hand, connecting words along the path of kindness.
**Tanka (original)**: 「ためらいて  
　それでも差し出す  
　手のぬくみ  
　ことばをつなぐ  
　やさしさの道」
