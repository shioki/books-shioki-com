### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through a lively Edo marketplace at dawn, observing merchants engrossed in their work with abacuses and ledgers. She tilts her head in contemplation, questioning why some labor so diligently despite minimal profits. The background features wooden shop signs and a misty morning atmosphere.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In a softly lit study filled with scrolls and foreign books, the townsgirl reads a Dutch book. An older scholar, resembling her imagined mentor Hashimoto Tsutomu, appears beside her. He is calm and thoughtful, with neatly tied hair and gentle eyes, pointing to a diagram that connects "ethics" with "wealth," explaining that diligence and restraint are the moral foundations of prosperity.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: Motivated by her newfound inspiration, the townsgirl organizes a small community lending circle, promoting honesty and collaboration among her neighbors. The scene captures her joy as she coordinates the townsfolk, her sleeves fluttering while she holds an abacus. A humorous moment occurs when someone brings sweets to donate, and she cheerfully reminds them, “Don’t forget moderation!”
**Dialogue**: “Don’t forget moderation!”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As night descends, the townsgirl kneels beside a paper lantern, brush in hand, composing a waka on a hanging tanzaku. Her expression radiates serenity and enlightenment as she contemplates the moral significance of work and generosity.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: “In working hands, light dwells and turns to wealth; it is discipline and trust that uphold the world.”  
**Tanka (original)**: 「働く手に　光宿りて　富となる　節と信こそ　世を支うなり」
