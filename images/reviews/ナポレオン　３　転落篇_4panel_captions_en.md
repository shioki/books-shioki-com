### Panel 1
**Scene**: In the lively streets of Edo, the townsgirl gazes curiously at a foreign illustration of a crowned man on horseback displayed at a rangaku merchant's stall. She tilts her head, contemplating how someone who once led a revolution could later become an emperor. The background is filled with townspeople chatting, a swaying paper lantern, and the faint sound of temple bells, reflecting her mixed feelings of curiosity and unease.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a dimly lit terakoya, the townsgirl is engrossed in a Dutch-translated book about Western rulers. Behind her stands a scholarly elder resembling author Sato Kenichi, depicted as a calm and thoughtful man with neatly tied back hair, dressed in simple scholar's robes. His intelligent and reflective gaze accompanies his soft explanation about the loneliness that comes with absolute power, blending analysis with compassion. The background is lined with shelves of scrolls and Dutch texts.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl is experimenting with a small abacus and parchment, mapping the rise and fall of an imaginary empire as if it were a mathematical curve. Ink splatters dramatically as she realizes that even perfect equations cannot capture the complexities of the human heart. A humorous moment occurs when her cat steps on her parchment, leaving paw prints across her "empire," symbolizing the unpredictable nature of fate.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At night, illuminated by the flickering light of an oil lamp, the townsgirl writes a waka on a narrow tanzaku. Her expression is serene yet reflective, her eyes distant as if she is contemplating the irony of history and the eternal cycle of memory and power. The final frame focuses on the brushstroke of her poem, softly glowing in the monochrome ink wash.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: Chasing ideals,  
Hands grasping for light,  
Unseeing shadows,  
At the end of loneliness,  
Memories remain.  
**Tanka (original)**: 「理想追い  
　光を掴む手  
　影を見ず  
　孤独の果てに  
　記憶ぞ残る」
