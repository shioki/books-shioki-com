### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through a lively Edo street filled with merchants and workers. She notices a craftsman lamenting his reduced pay while a wealthy merchant boasts about his profits. The girl pauses, slightly frowning, sensing the unfairness of “people treated like costs.” The background features wooden signboards with various trade goods, carpenters at work, and townsfolk exchanging coins.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her small room illuminated by candlelight, the townsgirl studies a Dutch economics manuscript and a thick arithmetic book. Beside her sits her mentor, an imagined figure resembling the author "相場英雄": a thoughtful middle-aged man with sharp eyes and tied-back hair, dressed in a simple scholar’s kimono. He gestures towards a diagram illustrating the gears of commerce, discussing the “truth hidden behind profit.” The girl listens intently, her eyes wide.
**Dialogue**: "There is a truth hidden behind profit."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The next day at the market, the townsgirl showcases her understanding by drawing a small abacus diagram in the dirt, explaining how “each person is a node in a living equation.” Townsfolk gather around, surprised by her insight. One laborer smiles, visibly moved. The girl’s expression is bright and confident, with a hint of mischief, as if she is turning knowledge into a quiet rebellion.
**Dialogue**: "Each person is a node in a living equation."

### Panel 4
**Scene**: In the evening, the townsgirl sits by the riverside, brush in hand, gazing at the reflection of lanterns on the water. She writes a waka on a tanzaku, capturing the essence of her learning — the dignity of people beyond the price of things. The scene is serene, with soft ink shading and deep emotional resonance.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: The worth of people,  
Measured in numbers in this world,  
I gaze upon it,  
With a heart that lights the way,  
Determined not to extinguish.  
**Tanka (original)**: 人の価（あたい）  
数に置きかふ世を  
見つめつつ  
灯（ひ）ともす心  
消さじとぞ思ふ
