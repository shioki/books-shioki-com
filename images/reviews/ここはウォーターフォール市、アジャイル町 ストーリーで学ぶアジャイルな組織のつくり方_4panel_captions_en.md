### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through a lively Edo marketplace, holding a folded scroll. She overhears merchants arguing about whose bookkeeping method is correct, pausing in confusion as she reflects on how “the world changes faster than rules can follow.” The background features wooden signboards, shops, and townsfolk bustling around.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her candlelit study, the townsgirl opens a Dutch-bound book titled “アジャイル町記.” Two imagined scholars appear on the pages—one is a calm, bespectacled thinker, while the other is a gentle, round-faced mentor with soft eyes. They discuss the spirit of “change and learning together,” depicted as ink-brushed portraits on the scroll. The townsgirl listens intently, brush in hand.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl gathers local craftspeople in the temple courtyard, drawing a grid on the ground with chalk to create a “task board” for their weaving and dyeing projects. Each artisan adds small wooden tags for their tasks, and when one drops his tag, others laugh and help him pick it up, symbolizing shared progress. The perspective is dynamic from above, showcasing cooperation and energy.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At sunset, the townsgirl sits by the riverbank, gazing at the rippling water that reflects lantern light. She writes a waka on a small tanzaku card, her expression serene yet inspired.  
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: As the flow changes, I entrust myself to it, learning together the town's resilience.  
**Tanka (original)**: 変わりゆく  
流れに身をも  
あずけつつ  
ともに学べる  
町のしなやかさ
