### Panel 1
**Scene**: In the bustling streets of Edo at dusk, the townsgirl observes two friends arguing near a teahouse, pondering the meaning of “true friendship” amidst the lively sounds of merchants and lanterns.
**Dialogue**: "What does true friendship mean? Is it duty, affection, or freedom?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her candlelit study, the townsgirl reads a philosophical book in kanbun and Dutch. A serene portrait of the imagined author, Toya Hiroshi, appears, as he explains the nature of friendship.
**Dialogue**: “Friendship is freedom bound by respect.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The next morning, under blooming plum trees, the townsgirl shares tea with a close friend. When her friend reveals a mistake in tears, the townsgirl takes her hand, creating a warm, silent understanding.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Alone in her room at night, the townsgirl writes a waka on a hanging scroll, her expression calm and radiant. The flickering candlelight casts a gentle glow on her inkstone.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]
**Tanka (translation)**: “To be with a friend — in that gentle sway of freedom — there lies the bond that unites us.”
**Tanka (original)**: 友といて　自由に揺らぐ　心こそ　われらを結ぶ　絆なるらむ
