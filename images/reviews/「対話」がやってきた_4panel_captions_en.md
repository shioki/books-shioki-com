### Panel 1
**Scene**: In a lively Edo-period merchant workshop, a curious townsgirl observes a stern guild headman inviting artisans to speak openly. An apprentice timidly points out a flaw in a product but is immediately burdened with responsibilities and blame, while others avoid eye contact. The townsgirl, holding a small waka notebook, watches with puzzled curiosity as the atmosphere grows tense, with various tools and items hinting at the pressures of silence.

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In a candlelit terakoya study room, the townsgirl studies a Dutch-learning book alongside counting rods and an abacus. An imagined mentor, resembling a scholarly woman from the Edo period, uses counting rods and tokens to illustrate that silence is rational when one person bears all the consequences. A visual diagram made of various items shows that the workplace atmosphere is shaped by rules and workload, rather than being a mysterious phenomenon.

### Panel 3
**Scene**: In an Edo guild meeting, the townsgirl boldly proposes a new system for handling complaints. A clever wooden mechanism redistributes responsibilities among officials instead of overwhelming the speaker. An artisan raises a hand about a defective crate, and the mechanism humorously launches tools and documents to the appropriate officials, leaving the reporter unharmed. The headman is humorously buried under paperwork, while the townsgirl smiles at the newfound visibility of hidden problems.

### Panel 4
**Scene**: In the quiet of an evening beside a lattice window, the townsgirl sits serenely with her brush, surrounded by her waka notebook and Dutch book. Behind her, faint echoes of workers speaking freely dissolve into the sky. Her expression reflects compassionate insight, suggesting that dialogue can thrive only when the system supports those who speak. 
**Tanka (translation)**: By raising our voices,  
A system that protects,  
We build dialogue,  
The environment flourishes,  
And our words take root.  
**Tanka (original)**: 声あげて  
損せぬ仕組み  
先に組む  
対話の器  
風土は実る
