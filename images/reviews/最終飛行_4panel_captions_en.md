### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl stands on a bridge at dusk in Edo, gazing at migrating birds flying into the fading sky. She reflects on the feelings of flight—freedom intertwined with fear. The river mirrors the clouds, creating a serene yet melancholic atmosphere. Her expression shows a mix of curiosity and awe.
**Dialogue**: "What must it feel like to soar through the sky?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her small study illuminated by a paper lantern, the townsgirl reads a Dutch book about flight while sketching bird wings next to her abacus. A portrait of a scholarly man, imagined as author Sato Kenichi, hangs on the shelf. He appears as a calm, thoughtful middle-aged man with tied-back hair and a neat beard, exuding a teacherly and philosophical presence.
**Dialogue**: "I wonder if he would understand my passion for flight."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl attaches paper wings to a bamboo kite and releases it from a hill overlooking Edo. The kite ascends high, trembling in the wind before breaking free and disappearing into the clouds. She watches silently, her hair and sleeves caught in the same breeze, realizing that flight is both a pursuit and a letting go.
**Dialogue**: "Fly high, little kite. Find your freedom."

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Kneeling by a small writing desk with a brush in hand, the townsgirl composes a tanka on a hanging scroll. The scene is tranquil, with moonlight streaming through the shoji. The kite string lies beside her, cut and coiled.
**Tanka (translation)**: At the edge of the sky,  
To return is the brave,  
Such is life,  
Entrusted to the wind,  
A noble flight.  
**Tanka (original)**: 空の果て  
還るは勇き  
いのちかな  
風に託して  
尊き飛行
