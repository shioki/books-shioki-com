### Panel 1
**Scene**: In a quiet backstreet of Edo, the townsgirl stops by a well, observing an elderly woman struggling to lift a bucket. She ponders the meaning of aging, with morning light shimmering on the water's surface, symbolizing both youth and age.
**Dialogue**: “What does it mean to grow old?”

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her small study, the townsgirl opens a Dutch book on natural philosophy, accompanied by a handwritten letter from two wise women. Imagined portraits of the authors 吉本由美 and 田尻久子 appear as ethereal figures in the margins, offering wisdom across time.
**Dialogue**: “Find joy in the changing seasons of the body.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: After a sudden rain, the townsgirl walks through the town, slipping slightly on the wet stone path. She laughs at herself and helps an old man pick up his fallen basket, their laughter echoing like the rhythm of life. A cat observes them from a roof tile.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]

### Panel 4
**Scene**: That evening, sitting by a paper lantern with a brush in hand, the townsgirl composes a waka on a narrow strip of paper. The ink softly bleeds into the fibers as she writes, her eyes shining with understanding.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]
**Tanka (translation)**: "Embracing the days of aging, with a heart that finds joy, the moon shines on the rhythm of life."
**Tanka (original)**: 「老いの日を  
　面白がりて  
　笑む心  
　かわりばんこの  
　月も照らせり」
