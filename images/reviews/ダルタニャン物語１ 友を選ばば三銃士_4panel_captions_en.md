### Panel 1
**Scene**: In the lively streets of Edo, the townsgirl observes a group of young samurai engaged in a heated debate about “honor” and “loyalty.” She tilts her head in curiosity, holding her brush and notebook tightly. The background features merchant stalls and a dojo banner fluttering in the breeze, symbolizing the conflict between ideals and reality.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her cozy study illuminated by candlelight, she opens a translated Western adventure book. A dreamlike vision emerges from the pages: a tall, confident man with curly hair and a flamboyant cloak—an imagined portrayal of Alexandre Dumas—converses with a calm, scholarly figure in traditional Japanese robes and round spectacles, representing Suzuki Rikiie. They appear to be debating friendship and loyalty while she listens intently, her eyes sparkling with excitement.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: Inspired by her reading, she steps outside into a light drizzle, where two neighbors are arguing over spilled sake. With a confident smile, she dramatically breaks her fan in two and raises it like a sword, playfully declaring that “true honor lies in trust, not titles.” The surprised men pause, then burst into laughter, shaking hands. Her action is both humorous and deeply meaningful.
**Dialogue**: “True honor lies in trust, not titles.”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: That evening, under the soft glow of a paper lantern, she writes a waka on a hanging tanzaku, gazing thoughtfully at the night sky. The elegant brushstrokes of her poem read:  
「折れし剣　誇りを掲げ　友と行く　理想と現の　はざま照らして」  
The scene conveys a gentle silence, resonating with themes of courage and friendship as they blend into the ink-washed night.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "A broken sword, raising pride, walks with friends, illuminating the gap between ideals and reality."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「折れし剣　誇りを掲げ　友と行く　理想と現の　はざま照らして」
