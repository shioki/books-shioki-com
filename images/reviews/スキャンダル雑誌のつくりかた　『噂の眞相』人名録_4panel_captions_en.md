### Panel 1
**Scene**: On a bustling Edo street, officials cover a troubling news sheet with a bland notice while townspeople discreetly look away. The young townsgirl notices a small corner of hidden evidence peeking out from beneath the paper, tilting her head in curiosity about the silence around her.
**Dialogue**: None.

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a cramped study filled with maps, an abacus, and various books, the townsgirl methodically investigates. She compares news sheets, organizes them chronologically, and checks claims against records, her expression shifting from curiosity to focused understanding.
**Dialogue**: None.

### Panel 3
**Scene**: In a lively Edo printing workshop, the townsgirl applies her knowledge. She stops a townsman from distributing an unverified sheet while dramatically pulling a printing-press lever after verifying the information. Verified pages burst out, knocking over officials' screens, showcasing her determination.
**Dialogue**: None.

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At dusk by a quiet canal, the townsgirl kneels with a brush, composing a waka. Behind her, newly printed sheets circulate among townspeople, while powerful men retreat into mist. Her expression reflects awe, completing her journey from curiosity to poetic contemplation.
**Tanka (translation)**: Splitting the darkness,  
Voices emerge into the world,  
The brush's resolve,  
Illuminating silence,  
Daring to question authority.  
**Tanka (original)**: 闇を裂き  
声を世に出す  
筆の意地  
黙る権威を  
照らして問わん
