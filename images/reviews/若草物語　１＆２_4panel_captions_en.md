### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through a lively Edo street, observing mothers and daughters enjoying rice balls outside a shop. She pauses, her expression contemplative, reflecting on how some pursue wealth while others find contentment in simplicity. The background features wooden houses, paper lanterns, and the soft glow of evening light filtering through.
**Dialogue**: "Some seek wealth, while others seem content with little..."

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her small study illuminated by an oil lamp, the townsgirl is engrossed in a thick foreign book titled “Little Women.” Two ghostly figures appear in the margins of an illustration — Louisa May Alcott depicted as a serene Western woman with mid-length curly hair and intelligent eyes, and Yumiko Taniguchi as a scholarly Japanese lady with a gentle face and traditional hairstyle. They whisper wisdom about effort, patience, and the quiet strength of women. The townsgirl listens intently, jotting down notes beside her abacus and Dutch dictionary.
**Dialogue**: "What wisdom they share about effort and patience..."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The following morning, the townsgirl assists a neighbor's child with writing. When ink spills, she chuckles softly, remarking that true learning often comes from small mistakes. The townspeople around her smile, inspired by her sincerity and joy in these simple moments. A playful cat tugs at her kimono hem, adding a touch of humor to the scene.
**Dialogue**: "True learning comes from small mistakes!"

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As dusk settles, the townsgirl sits by the riverbank, brush in hand, composing a waka on a small tanzaku slip. The wind gently tousles her hair as she gazes at the sky, contemplating the essence of true happiness. The tanzaku displays her poem in elegant calligraphy:  
"With modesty, I let my heart's flower bloom,  
Knowing a happiness deeper than wealth."  
**Tanka (translation)**: "With modesty, I let my heart's flower bloom, knowing a happiness deeper than wealth."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「つつましく　心の花を　咲かせつつ　富より深き　幸を知るなり」
