### Panel 1
**Scene**: In an Edo street near a temple school, the townsgirl pauses while practicing calligraphy, gazing at her half-written paper. She reflects on the haziness of her thoughts despite her swift brush movements, surrounded by bustling townsfolk and fluttering paper charms.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a small study filled with Dutch and Japanese scrolls, the townsgirl reads a rangaku-style book. The author’s portrait resembles a calm, scholarly man with short hair and intelligent eyes, exuding a composed aura. He appears as a spirit-like mentor, gesturing toward the written words.  
**Dialogue**: “Write it down, and reality will respond.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl steps outside under the night sky, holding a brush and notebook. She writes her aspirations to master arithmetic and explore the unknown. As she writes, the wind swirls the pages, and faint glowing characters rise into the air, bridging her thoughts and the real world.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Morning light fills her room as the townsgirl sits by the window, finishing a short waka poem with her brush. A small smile of understanding appears on her face.  
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "When I write my thoughts, entrusting them to the brush, a path opens up. The words sprout and become reality."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「思い書き  
　筆に託せば  
　道ひらく  
　言の葉芽吹き  
　現（うつつ）となりぬ」
