### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through the Edo town at dusk, observing scribes hurriedly copying documents. She pauses with a slight frown, murmuring about how people treat their writing as mere labor, devoid of thought. A temple school’s lantern in the background glows with the character for "calculation," creating an atmosphere of curiosity and quiet questioning.
**Dialogue**: "Why do they see writing as just work, without thought?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a small candlelit study, the townsgirl sits in front of an open Dutch book and a manuscript titled “Claude Code.” A calm, scholarly elder with gentle eyes and neatly tied hair appears beside her, dressed in simple robes. He gestures toward the manuscript, explaining the nature of AI.
**Dialogue**: "AI is another engineer — not a servant."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl is now in a lively scene, speaking to a small wooden automaton she built, as if they are collaborating on a design. Scrolls and abacuses are scattered around. The automaton gestures back, humorously "refusing" her first idea, prompting her to laugh and realize she needs to refine her thought process.
**Dialogue**: "Oh, you won’t accept that? I see, I need to think deeper!"

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At night, the townsgirl kneels by the window, her face illuminated by moonlight as she writes a waka on a narrow tanzaku strip. Her ink brush flows smoothly, capturing the essence of the book’s message.
