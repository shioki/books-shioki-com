### Panel 1
**Scene**: In the busy streets of Edo, the townsgirl kneels next to a notice board filled with conflicting announcements from officials and merchants. She looks puzzled, her head tilted, surrounded by the noisy chatter of townsfolk gossiping behind her.
**Dialogue**: “Truths multiply, yet none align.”

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In a serene temple study, the townsgirl is absorbed in reading a Dutch book next to a scroll with philosophical notes. A painting of a thoughtful, middle-aged scholar, imagined as Yomota Inuhiko, hangs on the wall. She bows slightly toward the portrait, reflecting on the courage to express herself in a foreign language.
**Dialogue**: “The courage to speak in a different tongue.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: At night in the marketplace, she boldly recites a poetic riddle that contradicts the crowd's chant. The people around her stop, surprised, but soon smile, recognizing her sincerity. The ink wash art captures the flickering lanterns and the quiet bravery on her face.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Back in her room, she writes by candlelight, her brush gliding slowly across the paper. Her expression is calm and focused. A short waka elegantly appears in the corner of a hanging scroll.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]
