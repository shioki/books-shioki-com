### Panel 1
**Scene**: In a bustling Edo streetscape filled with merchants and townsfolk, the townsgirl listens thoughtfully to her neighbors complaining about the difficulties of getting help from a local service clerk. She tilts her head, sensing the burden of customers, with a teahouse and paper lanterns in the background. Her expression shows a mix of curiosity and empathy.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a candle-lit study room filled with scrolls and Dutch books, the townsgirl reads a mysterious new treatise titled "The Book of Value Hubs." A scholarly figure resembling the book's author appears as a spirit-like teacher, gesturing to diagrams of interconnected circles symbolizing "customer voice" and "value creation." The townsgirl watches in awe, holding an ink brush.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: At a local merchant stall, the townsgirl applies her newfound insight. Using an abacus and clever signage, she reorganizes the flow so customers can get what they need faster and without confusion. The astonished customers smile in relief as she proudly explains her approach.
**Dialogue**: “Reducing their handoma brings true satisfaction.”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At dusk by the riverbank, the townsgirl kneels with a brush, composing a waka poem on a tanzaku strip fluttering in the breeze. Fireflies glimmer around her. Her calm smile reflects her understanding that both people and knowledge must harmonize.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "Voices resonate, / Unraveling the effort, / Opening the path, / With people and wisdom, / Let value bloom forth."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「声ひびき  
　手間をほどきて  
　道ひらく  
　人と知恵とで  
　価値を咲かせむ」
