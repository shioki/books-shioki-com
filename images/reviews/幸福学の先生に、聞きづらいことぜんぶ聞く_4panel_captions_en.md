### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a candle-lit study, the townsgirl reads a Dutch-translated manuscript on “the science of happiness,” her eyes sparkling with curiosity. A scroll nearby displays the portrait of an older scholar — a calm man in his sixties with neatly tied hair and a gentle smile, embodying the scholarly aura of Professor Takashi Maeno. He seems to softly convey the four elements of happiness: “Try it,” “Be thankful,” “It will work out,” “Be yourself.”
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl applies her newfound knowledge by sharing her mistakes with friends at a riverside teahouse, where they all laugh together in gratitude. One friend accidentally spills tea, but they all find humor in the situation. The townsgirl raises her fan and cheerfully says, “It will work out,” spreading warmth and optimism. The composition captures dynamic motion and laughter through bold sumi-e brushstrokes.
**Dialogue**: “It will work out.”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Night has fallen. The townsgirl sits beside a paper lamp, brush in hand, composing a waka on a small tanzaku slip. The atmosphere is quiet and luminous, showcasing her serene smile as she contemplates the flow of life. The short poem appears in elegant Japanese calligraphy.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "Try it out, / In the days of gratitude, / The heart clears up, / It will work out."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「  
やってみて　  
ありがとう言う　日々のなか　  
なんとかなると　心は晴れる  
」
