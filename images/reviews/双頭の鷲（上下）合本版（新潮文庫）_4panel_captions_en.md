### Panel 1
**Scene**: On a lively Edo street, the young townsgirl pauses amid the bustle, watching samurai argue heatedly about “honor” and “duty.” She tilts her head, notebook in hand, her eyes full of curiosity. Around her, merchants and townsfolk whisper about a distant war. Her thoughtful gaze suggests she’s wondering whether conflict can ever serve something greater than pride.  
**Dialogue**: (thinking) “Honor… duty… but to what end?”

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her small study, surrounded by both Japanese and foreign books, she reads intently under candlelight. From the flickering glow, the vision of a calm, scholarly mentor appears — a middle-aged man in a simple kimono, explaining with gentle authority as he points to a hand-drawn map of Europe. He speaks of how battles once fought for personal glory gave way to struggles that shaped nations. The girl listens in awe, brush frozen above the page.  
**Dialogue**: Mentor (softly): “When reason meets purpose, even war transforms its meaning.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: In the bustling marketplace, the townsgirl now applies what she’s learned. With an abacus and compass, she helps townspeople organize supplies efficiently, her movements precise yet kind. Her sleeves flow as she balances logic and empathy. An elderly man watches, astonished by her clarity and calm.  
**Dialogue**: Old man: “Such order… from one so young!”  
Townsgirl (smiling): “For everyone’s sake, numbers can serve the heart too.”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At night, she kneels by the window, moonlight spilling across her desk. With quiet focus, she writes a tanka, her brush gliding over the paper. The poem captures her realization — that reason, emotion, and purpose are threads woven through the fabric of history.  
**Dialogue**: (whispering) “May my small light reach beyond myself…”  
**Tanka (translation)**:  
Reason and feeling,  
burning as one through time’s veil—  
even a lone light  
can pierce the dark of the age  
and shine upon a nation.  
**Tanka (original)**:  
理と情　  
ひとつに燃えて　  
時を裂く  
孤の光も　  
国を照らせり
