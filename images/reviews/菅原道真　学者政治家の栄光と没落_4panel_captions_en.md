### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl stands thoughtfully in front of a temple gate, pondering whether knowledge should be revered or utilized, while the bustling Edo streets and fluttering paper charms create a lively backdrop.

### Panel 2

**Dialogue**: “Knowledge should serve the world.”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As the sun sets, the townsgirl kneels by the riverbank, writing a waka on a tanzaku, her smile reflecting the beauty of the moment as the sky's colors ripple across the inked paper.  
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: To learn is to be a light that illuminates the world; if you ask the written word, see the heart of a person, not a god.  
**Tanka (original)**: 「学ぶとは　世を照らす灯（ひ）ぞ　書に問わば  
　神ならぬ人の　志（こころ）を見よ」
