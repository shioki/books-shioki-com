### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl stands in front of a moneylender's stall on a busy Edo street, observing people borrowing and lending money carelessly. She tilts her head in confusion, hearing phrases like "interest disappearing" and "easy profits." The background features wooden signboards and merchants counting coins, while her expression reflects both curiosity and unease, sensing something deeper is wrong beneath the surface of prosperity.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her modest candlelit room, the townsgirl studies a Dutch economic manuscript alongside scrolls of wasan (Japanese mathematics). A portrait of a thoughtful middle-aged man, representing the author, hangs on the wall. His features are imagined as calm and sharp-eyed, with tied hair and a serious demeanor, seemingly inviting her to question the ethics behind numbers. The air is filled with the scent of ink and a quiet tension.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl visits a local rice exchange, applying her knowledge. She draws a chalk diagram on the ground, explaining to astonished traders that "when value loses its measure, trust collapses." Her brush moves dynamically through the air as coins scatter, creating an intense, almost surreal moment where knowledge meets reality. The composition is bold and kinetic, showcasing both intellect and moral struggle.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At night, the townsgirl kneels by the riverbank under the moonlight, writing a waka on a small paper strip. Her reflection ripples in the water, conveying a serene yet pensive mood.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "When the sound of coin fades, hearts turn hollow; the thread of trust snaps, and the path is lost."  
**Tanka (original)**: 金の音　消えて心も　空となり  
　信の糸切れ　道を見失う
