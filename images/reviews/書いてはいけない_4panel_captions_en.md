### Panel 1
**Scene**: In a lively Edo street filled with paper lanterns and news criers, the townsgirl pauses in front of a notice board that is conspicuously empty. She tilts her head, sensing that something is being concealed from the public. The atmosphere is filled with curiosity and a hint of unease, as townsfolk whisper around her.

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a dimly lit study illuminated by candlelight, the townsgirl is engrossed in reading a forbidden Dutch manuscript alongside a mysterious new book authored by a scholar resembling Morinaga Takuro. This gentle, middle-aged man with round glasses exudes a calm yet serious demeanor, embodying quiet defiance. He appears either as a portrait on a hanging scroll or as a guiding teacher figure, radiating moral conviction through his presence. The inked shadows around them suggest the heavy burden of truth against the backdrop of silence.

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl ascends to a rooftop under the night sky, scattering folded paper cranes that drift through the darkness, each carrying written words of truth. Below, surprised townspeople gaze upward as the papers flutter down. Her determined expression is illuminated by the moonlight, symbolizing her courage to speak out when others choose to remain silent.

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As dawn breaks over Edo, the townsgirl kneels at her writing desk, brush in hand, composing a tanka on a hanging scroll. Her expression is calm yet resolute, reflecting her commitment to truth. 
**Tanka (translation)**: In a world frightened by silence, I raise my voice, illuminating the truth with the light of my brush.
**Tanka (original)**: 「沈黙に  
　怯えし世にも  
　声あげて  
　真実（まこと）を照らす  
　筆のひかりよ」
