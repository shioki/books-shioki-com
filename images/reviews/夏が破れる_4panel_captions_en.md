**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through a bustling Edo street in midsummer, wiping sweat from her brow as she observes a heated argument among merchants over profits. A stray dog watches quietly from a distance, and she pauses, feeling an unsettling tension in the air—“why does wealth bring such cruelty?” The scene is illuminated by harsh summer light, highlighting the stark contrasts of ink and evoking the underlying tension of daily life.
