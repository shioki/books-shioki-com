### Panel 1
**Scene**: At dusk on a lively Edo street, the young townsgirl pauses before a craftsman’s stall where new gadgets and polished abaci glimmer under the lantern light. She looks puzzled, wondering why her town’s inventions never seem to change, even as she overhears rumors from Nagasaki about a distant city where ideas become real overnight. Merchants are closing their shops, and paper lanterns sway gently in the evening breeze.  
**Dialogue**: “Why do things here stay the same… while elsewhere, ideas take flight so quickly?”

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a dimly lit temple school, she studies a Dutch mechanical treatise. In her imagination, six scholars appear—wise mentors with distinct faces: one calm behind narrow spectacles, another sharp-eyed and poised, a young artisan with ink-stained hands, a bearded tinkerer smiling warmly, a slender reader reciting aloud, and a broad-shouldered translator radiating confidence. They seem to discuss the principle of “making before perfecting,” their presence guiding her thoughts.  
**Dialogue**: “To create first, and refine later… perhaps that is the true craft.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: In her courtyard, sleeves tied up, the townsgirl builds a small wooden automaton bird from scrap metal and bamboo gears. Children gather around, wide-eyed, as the bird flaps its wings. Ink lines burst with motion and laughter. She smiles, realizing that by moving her hands, her mind begins to move too.  
**Dialogue**: “When the hands move, the mind follows.”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: Night falls. By candlelight, she gazes at her half-finished automaton, a gentle smile on her face. She takes up her brush and writes a tanka on a hanging scroll—its strokes bold and fluid, embodying her spirit of endless experimentation.  
**Dialogue**: (softly) “Still unfinished… yet already opening the future.”  
**Tanka (translation)**:  
With every trial,  
hands turn thought into form—  
unfinished still,  
yet in each imperfection  
the future begins to open.  
**Tanka (original)**:  
手を動かし  
思ひを形に  
試すたび  
未完のままで  
未来ひらけり
