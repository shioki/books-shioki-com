### Panel 4
**Scene**: Night falls as the townsgirl sits by the riverbank, writing a tanka under the moonlight. The paper glows softly with sumi ink wash, and her peaceful smile reflects the harmony between economy and humanity.
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: Working brings forth smiles more than profits; the warmth of people surpasses wealth.
