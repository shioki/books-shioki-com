### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through a bustling Edo street at dusk, observing craftsmen and merchants sharing laughter. She pauses, tilting her head in contemplation, pondering how connections, rather than rules, seem to help people thrive. The background features wooden signboards and softly glowing paper lanterns.  
**Dialogue**: "Isn't it fascinating how connections bind us beyond reason?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In a small, candlelit rangaku study room, the townsgirl studies a foreign manuscript. The book illustrates a portrait of a man resembling Noritake Kinashi, an older gentleman with expressive wrinkles, lively eyes, and a carefree smile, dressed in a loose robe with a simple wristwatch-like ornament. His presence radiates warmth and creativity.  
**Dialogue**: "He speaks of freedom with responsibility... trusting passion over logic."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl engages a group of children in the street, encouraging them to try a new counting game she created from her arithmetic studies. Although the children initially struggle and laugh, she cheerfully reassures them that failure is merely "checking the path." Her animated pose, with sleeves fluttering, conveys fearless action and shared joy.  
**Dialogue**: "Don't worry! Failure is just a step on the way!"

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As night falls, the townsgirl sits beside a paper lantern, dipping her brush into ink. She smiles gently, her eyes reflecting the moonlight, as she writes a tanka on a hanging strip of paper beside her.  
**Tanka (translation)**: "Even failure, wrapped in laughter, becomes a bond;  
In freedom, we find our way to live."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「失敗も  
　笑いで包み  
　縁となる  
　自由の中に  
　人は生きゆく」
