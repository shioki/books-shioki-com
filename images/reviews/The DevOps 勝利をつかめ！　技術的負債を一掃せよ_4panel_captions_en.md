### Panel 1
**Scene**: In a bustling Edo town street filled with shops and wooden signboards, the townsgirl observes her teacher struggling with a chaotic mess of abacus calculations and scrolls, representing "technical debt." She tilts her head in curiosity at the tangled ropes connecting the ledgers.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a candlelit study cluttered with Dutch and Japanese texts, the townsgirl reads a newly imported rangaku scroll. From her imagination, three mentors appear: Gene Kim, Akira Sakakibara, and Takahiro Nagao, discussing how “simplicity and learning” can alleviate the burden of complexity. The townsgirl listens intently, brush poised in the air.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: In a lively marketplace, the townsgirl puts her newfound knowledge into practice by reorganizing the ledger scrolls into smaller, labeled bundles, symbolizing modular teams. She cheers to the workers, encouraging collaboration and clarity as the previously tangled ropes become straight and orderly. A merchant looks surprised at the swift progress.  
**Dialogue**: “Let’s make the flow visible!”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At dusk on a bridge, the townsgirl gazes at the flowing river, brush in hand, composing a waka on a small tanzaku paper. The ink flows gently like the water, capturing the essence of speed, learning, and clarity from freeing oneself of debt.  
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: "Watching the flow, / A heart free from stagnation, / Learning and speed unite, / The power that binds us all."  
**Tanka (original)**: 「流れ見て  
　滞りなき  
　心かな  
　学びと速さ  
　結ぶ力よ」
