The background features wooden houses and signboards, capturing the vibrant daily life of the period. She holds a small brush and notebook, her expression thoughtful.

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In her dimly lit room at night, the townsgirl studies a worn rangaku book and a wasan scroll. A portrait of an elderly scholar, reminiscent of Deguchi Haruaki, appears in the book's margins. He is depicted as a wise man in his seventies, with kind eyes and scholarly robes, his expression gentle yet sharp, as if imparting knowledge about history, the world, and evidence.

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The following morning, the townsgirl gathers children and townsfolk by a well, using pebbles and bamboo sticks to demonstrate "thinking for oneself." She shows them how to measure the well's depth through reasoning and observation, and the crowd reacts with amazement and laughter. Her energetic gestures bring the scene to life.  
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At dusk, the townsgirl sits by the riverbank, brush in hand, reflecting on the rippling water. She writes her waka on a hanging tanzaku, encapsulating the essence of her learning journey.  
**Dialogue**: None
