### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl strolls through a lively Edo street by the harbor, observing craftsmen as they repair a small ship. She tilts her head in contemplation, pondering how people refer to great victories as “miracles,” while questioning if there are hidden reasons rooted in skill and calculation. The background is filled with carpenters, merchants, and students from a terakoya, capturing the essence of craftsmanship and curiosity.
**Dialogue**: "They call great victories 'miracles,' but could there be hidden reasons in skill and calculation?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In a dimly lit study, the townsgirl studies a Dutch nautical chart and a thick engineering scroll intently. On the wall, a portrait of a calm, middle-aged man with scholarly eyes and a modest smile hangs—representing Yasuyuki Hata, envisioned as a thoughtful ship engineer. His image seems to convey wisdom, teaching her that history can be understood through time, space, and physical laws rather than emotions.
**Dialogue**: "History is read through time, space, and the laws of nature, not just emotions."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl is by a pond, experimenting with small wooden ships she has made. She adjusts their sails and measures their angles with a bamboo ruler, observing how the wind and balance affect their movement. Her face lights up with the joy of discovery as she realizes that even the “miracles” of history are constructed from knowledge, training, and logistics.
**Dialogue**: "Even history's 'miracles' are built on knowledge and careful planning."

### Panel 4
**Scene**: It is nighttime, and the townsgirl sits by the light of a lamp, with a brush and paper in front of her. She writes a waka on a small tanzaku, encapsulating the book’s central theme—that science and craftsmanship intertwine to tell Japan's true story.
**Dialogue**: "Craftsmanship and knowledge weave the true story of Japan."
**Tanka (translation)**: Craftsmanship builds history on waves of knowledge; miracles reveal the logic behind them.
**Tanka (original)**: ものづくり  
積む知の波に  
歴史あり  
奇跡の影に  
理を見つけぬ
