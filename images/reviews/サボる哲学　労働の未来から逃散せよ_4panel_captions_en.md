### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through a busy Edo street, observing laborers carrying goods and merchants shouting prices. She tilts her head, feeling a strange unease as everyone seems engrossed in their work. 
**Dialogue**: “Is working truly a good thing...?”

### Panel 2
**Scene**: In a quiet study, she opens a foreign book titled “The Philosophy of Slacking Off.” From the pages, a vision of the author appears, depicted as an older man with messy hair and sharp eyes, dressed in simple robes, smiling and gesturing.
**Dialogue**: “Laugh, deviate!”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: Feeling inspired, she walks to a riverside where townspeople are hauling nets. She suddenly flops down under a willow tree, humming and skipping stones. Her laughter is infectious, causing the workers to pause and join her in play.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As evening falls, the townsgirl kneels by lantern light, brush in hand, composing a waka on a small slip of paper. Her expression is peaceful, embodying her newfound freedom.
**Dialogue**: [No dialogue]
**Tanka (translation)**: "Without working,  
Listening to the wind  
In laughter's embrace,  
Free from control,  
My body becomes a cloud."
**Tanka (original)**: 「働かず  
笑いの中に  
風を聴く  
支配を離れ  
身は雲となる」
