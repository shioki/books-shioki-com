### Panel 1
**Scene**: In a narrow alley of an Edo town, the townsgirl stops to listen to a storyteller narrating the tale of Yamato Takeru. She looks puzzled as she hears about the hero who wore women's clothing to deceive his enemies. The background features paper lanterns, passing samurai, and the lively sounds of the evening market.
**Dialogue**: "He wore women's clothes?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a dimly lit study filled with scrolls and Dutch books, the townsgirl reads a cultural treatise. A wise scholar resembling Inoue Shōichi appears as her mentor, an elderly man in his seventies with short hair and a gentle gaze. He points to an open book that illustrates Yamato Takeru in female attire, speaking softly about "the beauty hidden in disguise."
**Dialogue**: "There is beauty in disguise."

### Panel 3
**Scene**: Inspired, the townsgirl disguises herself as a traveling performer to observe the festival crowd. Her cleverness and laughter captivate the onlookers. The panel captures her fluttering sleeves, the amused townsfolk, and her quick, darting eyes, blending intellect with playfulness.
**Dialogue**: "Look at her! So lively!"

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At dawn, she sits by the riverbank, brush in hand, composing a waka on a hanging scroll. The rising mist and soft ink strokes create a sense of quiet revelation.
**Dialogue**: "I will capture this moment."
**Tanka (translation)**: "As a woman,  
   adorned with wisdom,  
   a man stands forth.  
   Is beauty and strength  
   not found in anyone?"
**Tanka (original)**: 「女なる  
　智の装ひに  
　男立つ  
　美と力とは  
　ひとにあらずや」
