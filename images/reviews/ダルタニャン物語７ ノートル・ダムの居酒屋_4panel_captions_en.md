### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through an Edo street at dusk, listening to two merchants arguing about loyalty and profit. She pauses, thoughtfully gazing at the temple bell tower, murmuring about “responsibility and pride.” The atmosphere is calm yet reflective, with faintly glowing lanterns and traditional wooden houses in the background.

Their ghostly presence seems to silently debate about duty and honor as she takes notes.
**Dialogue**: (No dialogue)

### Panel 3
**Scene**: The townsgirl is now outside, mediating a quarrel between two samurai over a debt. Instead of scolding them, she smiles knowingly, using her abacus to fairly divide the amount, softly stating that “true strength lies in taking responsibility.” The dynamic brush strokes show her kimono fluttering, symbolizing the balance between reason and emotion.
**Dialogue**: "True strength lies in taking responsibility."

### Panel 4
**Scene**: At night, the townsgirl sits by the riverbank under the moon, writing a waka on a slip of paper. The wind ripples the water's surface as she gazes skyward, her expression serene yet resolute. The short poem appears vertically on the page.
**Dialogue**: (No dialogue)
**Tanka (translation)**: "To know apart from the seat of power is to understand one's duty; the flower of truth blooms in silence."
**Tanka (original)**: 「  
権の座を  
離れて知るは  
己が義
