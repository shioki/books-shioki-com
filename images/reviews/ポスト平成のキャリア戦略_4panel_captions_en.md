### Panel 1
**Scene**: In the lively streets of Edo, a young townsgirl pauses to observe a merchant and his apprentice arguing about the merits of “safe work over risky dreams.” She tilts her head, contemplating the deeper question of whether one can live both nobly and ambitiously. The background features a narrow street adorned with paper lanterns and a sign that reads “terakoya.”
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside the terakoya, illuminated by candlelight, the townsgirl studies thick scrolls filled with Dutch letters and arithmetic diagrams. Two imagined figures emerge from her books—one resembling Shiono Makoto, a composed scholar with sharp eyes and tied-back hair, and the other resembling Sasaki Norihiko, a younger thinker with slightly messy hair and an animated expression. They engage in a lively debate, embodying the concepts of “Hungry & Noble.” The girl listens intently, her ink brush ready.
**Dialogue**: None

### Panel 3
**Scene**: At dawn, the townsgirl applies her newfound knowledge by drawing chalk diagrams on the street, teaching children how the combination of different skills (arithmetic × Dutch studies × poetry) creates something unique. A passing samurai looks on in confusion as she cheerfully explains, “The path of multiplication expands the vessel of the heart!”
**Dialogue**: “掛け算の道は、心の器を広げること！”

### Panel 4
**Scene**: In the evening's tranquility, the townsgirl kneels beside a paper lamp, brush in hand, gazing at the stars reflected in a basin of water. On a hanging tanzaku, she writes a waka that encapsulates the essence of “Hungry & Noble” and the courage to challenge:
**Dialogue**: None  
**Tanka (translation)**: With determination, I layer my aspirations to open the path; even in hunger, I shall hold the light high.  
**Tanka (original)**: 志を　掛けて重ねて　道ひらく  
飢えても凛と　灯を掲げん
