### Panel 1
**Scene**: The townsgirl walks through a bustling Edo marketplace, pausing to listen to two merchants arguing about their differing opinions. She holds her abacus and notebook, looking puzzled by their disagreement.
**Dialogue**: "Why can't they just listen to each other?"

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a dimly lit temple study, the townsgirl studies foreign texts and maps. A calm scholar resembling Ryuuichi Kanenari sits nearby, guiding her with a gentle demeanor. He points to a scroll depicting distant lands.
**Dialogue**: “True understanding begins with listening.”

### Panel 3
**Scene**: At a riverside, townspeople are quarreling over the rising cost of rice. The townsgirl kneels and listens to both sides before using her abacus to propose a fair solution. The crowd is surprised by her calm approach.
**Dialogue**: "Let me show you a fair way."

### Panel 4
**Scene**: As evening falls, the townsgirl writes by the light of a lamp, her brush moving gracefully over washi paper. In a small tanzaku panel, her waka reflects the importance of listening and empathy.
**Tanka (translation)**: Listening builds a bridge of the heart; beyond the voices, there are lights of humanity.
**Tanka (original)**: 「聞くことは  
　心の橋を  
　架けること  
　声の向こうに  
　人の灯あり」
