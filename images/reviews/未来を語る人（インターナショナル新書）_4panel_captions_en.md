### Panel 1
**Scene**: In the lively streets of Edo, a young townsgirl stops at a merchant's stall filled with imported scrolls. She frowns thoughtfully as she listens to the townsfolk discussing the idea that "wealth and power rule all," while gazing at the river reflecting both grand mansions and humble boats. Cherry blossoms drift in the breeze as she quietly wonders, “Is there another way to measure prosperity?”  
**Dialogue**: “Is there another way to measure prosperity?”

### Panel 2
**Scene**: Inside a dimly lit study surrounded by scrolls and abacuses, the townsgirl opens a Dutch-bound book titled "The People Who Speak of the Future." From its pages, wise figures appear—portraits of world thinkers: Jared Diamond as a calm-eyed scholar, Kate Raworth sketching a circular "donut" diagram, Branko Milanović as a stern mathematician, and Thomas Sedlacek as a tousled-haired philosopher with a quill. Their ghostly forms engage in conversation above the book, illuminated by the soft lamplight, while the townsgirl listens with awe.

### Panel 3
**Scene**: At dawn, the townsgirl gathers neighborhood children in the temple yard, drawing a circular chart in the sand with a stick. She explains that the balance between “enough for all” and “care for nature” is essential. The children giggle and add pebbles labeled “kindness” and “learning.” A nearby merchant overhears, intrigued by her words. The townsgirl's eyes shine with determination—she has transformed abstract ideas into practical actions.

**Scene**: In the tranquil evening light, the townsgirl sits by the window, brush poised over a strip of washi paper. She gazes at the moon's reflection in her inkstone, smiling gently as she writes a waka that captures the essence of the book.
