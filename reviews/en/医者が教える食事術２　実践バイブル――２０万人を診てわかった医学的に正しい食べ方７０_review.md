## 🎯 The Core of This Book
At the heart of this book is a clear awareness of the problem that "carbohydrates are the root of modern diseases." The author, 牧田善二, asserts based on clinical data from over 200,000 patients that excessive carbohydrate intake leads to obesity, diabetes, heart disease, and even Alzheimer's disease. His stance, which overturns the conventional wisdom that demonizes fats, argues that fats are essential for health, redefining the field of nutrition.

Moreover, this book is not just a theoretical text but is structured as a "practical bible." It comprehensively addresses cooking methods, the order of eating, and how to undergo medical examinations, claiming that by honing the skill of "how to eat," one can change the quality of life. Readers are encouraged to shift their mindset from "choosing" meals to "designing" them.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **The Addictive Nature of Carbohydrates and Mood Control**  
  Carbohydrates cause a rapid spike in blood sugar, leading to temporary pleasure, but the subsequent crash creates further cravings. This is not merely a preference but a physiological dependency, scientifically explaining the "unbreakable eating habits" of modern individuals.

- **The Collapse of the "Calorie Myth"**  
  The idea that weight can be explained simply by the difference between calorie intake and expenditure is rejected. Calorie restriction that ignores metabolism and hormonal functions paradoxically puts the body into energy-saving mode, making it harder to lose weight.

- **The Relationship Between Fat Intake and Longevity**  
  The fear of fat itself is a health risk, and studies show that higher fat intake correlates with lower mortality rates. Fats are essential for maintaining brain function and hormones, challenging the stereotype that "fat equals bad."

- **Cooking Methods Affect Aging**  
  The book reveals that high-temperature cooking produces AGEs (advanced glycation end-products) that accelerate aging, suggesting cooking techniques like boiling, steaming, and using vinegar. The perspective that minor differences in how we eat can lead to significant long-term health disparities is refreshing.

- **The Importance of Scientific Literacy**  
  It advocates for not blindly accepting the "evidence" that the food industry selectively uses, urging readers to develop the ability to discern the quality of research. In an age overflowing with health information, having scientific thinking is a form of self-defense.

## 🔍 Relevance to Modern Context
In recent years, with the aging population, "extending healthy life expectancy" has become a national issue in Japan. The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare emphasizes the prevention of lifestyle-related diseases through a combination of diet and exercise, and concepts like carbohydrate restriction and AGE suppression are becoming mainstream. 牧田氏's proposals are positioned as practical approaches that go beyond public policy.

Additionally, media interest in "stress-free health methods" is rising, with a focus on practices that "reduce mistakes" rather than perfectionistic dietary restrictions. The "techniques of eating" presented in this book align perfectly with this trend. It offers a medical and realistic answer to the question of how to balance pleasure and health in a carbohydrate-dependent society, highlighting the contemporary value of this book.

---

## 🚀 Practical Suggestions
1. **Limit carbohydrate intake to under 100g per day**  
   Carbohydrate restriction is central to preventing obesity and diabetes, especially by reducing carbohydrates at night. Stabilizing blood sugar leads to stability in weight and mood.

2. **Don’t fear fats; consume quality oils**  
   By consuming appropriate amounts of olive oil, fish oil, and animal fats, one can balance hormones and metabolism. It is more important to assess the quality of fats than to restrict them.

3. **Change cooking methods**  
   Reduce fried and grilled foods, and incorporate methods like boiling, steaming, and using vinegar to suppress AGEs. This can help slow the aging process.

4. **Be mindful of the order of eating**  
   Eating in the order of "protein → vegetables → carbohydrates" can prevent rapid spikes in blood sugar. Small adjustments in order can contribute to diabetes prevention.

5. **Undergo regular detailed examinations**  
   Utilize tests like CT and MRI to detect cancers and heart diseases early. An active approach to adopting medical technology leads to true preventive healthcare.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『医者が教える食事術２　実践バイブル』 is not just a health book but a practical medical text that fundamentally questions modern people's "common sense about food." It translates themes like carbohydrate restriction and AGE suppression into actionable insights grounded in scientific evidence, bridging theory and practice.

In particular, the perspective of viewing eating methods, cooking techniques, and examination methods as an integrated whole offers a comprehensiveness not found in traditional nutrition texts. By redefining health as a "technique" rather than an "effort," this book provides a sustainable health strategy for busy modern individuals.
