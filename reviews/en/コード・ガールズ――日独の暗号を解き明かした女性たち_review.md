## 🎯 Core of the Book
*コード・ガールズ* is a nonfiction work that unveils the untold story of the women who served in top-secret codebreaking missions in the United States during World War II. These women excelled in mathematics and foreign languages, yet society at the time expected them to become teachers—and little else.  

When the war demanded their intellectual abilities, they stepped to the front lines of information warfare and achieved results that influenced the course of history. This book excavates the long-hidden history of their contributions and challenges the gendered structure of intellectual labor.  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Women’s intelligence shaped the war**  
  In the world of codebreaking, women’s analytical skills and intuition played a decisive role. The idea that victory depended not on bullets but on thought and perseverance redefines the value of intellectual work.  

- **The power of education to transform society**  
  The linguistic and logical skills honed through teacher training became invaluable in the new field of cryptanalysis. The book offers a compelling example of how education can transcend individual potential and lead to national achievements.  

- **Reevaluating women forgotten by history**  
  After the war, many of these accomplishments were credited to men, even though more than half of the work was done by women. This book seeks to restore these silent intellectual heroines to their rightful place in history.  

- **Between information warfare and ethics**  
  Decoding enemy communications inevitably involved ethical dilemmas. Through this, the book raises profound questions about the nature of information and the responsibility that comes with knowledge.  

- **The power of intellectual collaboration**  
  The women supported one another, shared knowledge, and achieved results together. The book emphasizes that it was not individual genius but collective intelligence that changed the course of history.  

---

## 🔍 Relevance to the Modern Context
Even today, women’s participation in STEM fields and information security remains a challenge. The “code girls” portrayed in this book vividly demonstrate the importance of exercising intellectual ability free from gender constraints.  

In the age of AI and big data, the ethics and responsibility of “reading” information are once again under scrutiny. The moral conflicts surrounding wartime codebreaking resonate with contemporary issues of data use and privacy.  

---

## 🚀 Practical Takeaways
- **Make women’s intellectual labor visible**  
  Recognizing the “unsung contributors” of the past reminds us to build systems today that record and share achievements transparently. Such visibility fosters role models for future generations.  

- **Embrace interdisciplinary learning**  
  Just as classical languages and linguistics proved useful in codebreaking, knowledge from outside one’s specialty can spark creative breakthroughs. Interdisciplinary learning nurtures flexible thinking.  

- **Cultivate a culture of patience and collaboration**  
  The code girls worked tirelessly through vast amounts of data. In modern intellectual work, sustained collaboration often yields deeper results than short-term gains.  

- **Strengthen education in information ethics**  
  Understanding the gravity of reading another’s communications is as relevant today as ever. Those who handle information must develop a strong ethical awareness of their responsibilities.  

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
*コード・ガールズ* not only reconstructs wartime history but also fundamentally reexamines the relationship between intellectual labor and gender. Author ライザ・マンディ, drawing on extensive research and testimonies, brings to life the voices of women who fought in silence.  

This book is essential reading for anyone interested in history, gender, or the information society. Readers will come away with a renewed sense of how intellect and courage can shape an era—and will be prompted to consider how we can continue to tell the stories of those whose contributions were never recorded.
