## 🎯 The Core of This Book
『自己紹介2.0』 presents a new concept that goes beyond the traditional "self-introduction to be remembered (1.0)" to "self-introduction to build trust and share the future (2.0)." The author, 横石崇, redefines self-introduction not merely as a formal greeting but as "the first step in building trust" and "a dialogue to discuss the future."

At the heart of this book is a future-oriented self-expression that starts with "Why." By speaking about what one wants to achieve in the future rather than past achievements, it draws out the expectations and empathy of the listener. Furthermore, it provides specific methods for deepening self-understanding through "seven tools" and creating value through cross-border and diversity, allowing readers to learn practical techniques to connect their "sense of purpose" with society.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Building Trust is the Essence of Self-Introduction**  
  The purpose of self-introduction lies not in "being remembered" but in "building trust." A sincere and future-oriented narrative is required as the first step in deepening relationships.

- **"Managing Expectations" is the Key to Success**  
  Avoiding excessive expectations from others and setting appropriate expectations generates trust. Drawing on Drucker's theories, it argues that "designing expectations" is at the core of communication.

- **Empathy is Created by Speaking from the Future**  
  Structuring self-introduction in the order of "future → past → present" evokes positive expectations in the listener. Sharing a vision for the future becomes the most powerful form of self-expression.

- **Independence Means "Increasing Dependency Sources"**  
  It presents a paradoxical view that true independence comes from broadening connections with others. A network based on trust expands individual freedom and possibilities.

- **Express Yourself with "Verbs" and "Hashtags"**  
  Instead of defining oneself by titles, express oneself through actions and show multifaceted identity with multiple hashtags. This allows for a balance between individuality and empathy.

---

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
The proposed "Self-Introduction 2.0" is deeply connected to the rapid changes in today's business environment. With the rise of side jobs and remote work, the ability to build trust quickly is essential; the power to convey one's value in one minute is a competitive advantage.

Moreover, as values shift from organization-centered to individual-centered, "individual trust" is becoming a new economic foundation. In a society where women's empowerment and respect for diversity are advancing, self-introduction functions not merely as a formality but as a "tool for designing relationships." 横石's proposals serve as practical guidelines at this turning point in the era.

---

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Construct Self-Introduction in the Order of "Future → Past → Present"**  
   Start by sharing your vision, then provide the background, and conclude with current actions. This order stimulates the listener's expectations and builds trust.

2. **Define Yourself with "Verbs" Instead of "Nouns"**  
   Use dynamic expressions like "a person who shapes the future" instead of "designer" to simultaneously gain impression and empathy.

3. **Tag Your Career with Hashtags**  
   Set tags from three perspectives: affiliation, passion, and skills, to visualize your multifaceted identity. This increases connections with others who share commonalities.

4. **Conduct Regular "Self-Inventory"**  
   Reflect on the origin of your name, influential people, and turning points to clarify your "Why." This becomes the foundation for a future-oriented self-introduction.

5. **Embrace Cross-Border Opportunities and Dive into New Environments**  
   Encounters with different fields and people create new value. Placing yourself in unknown environments sharpens both self-understanding and trust-building.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『自己紹介2.0』 is not just a book of self-promotion techniques but a practical guide that answers the fundamental question of "how to connect oneself with society." The author's suggestions present a new form of communication where individuals build trust with others by articulating their beliefs and futures.

Especially in today's rapidly changing society, clarifying one's sense of purpose and the ability to gain trust quickly is essential for all business professionals. This book serves as a reliable compass for those aiming to balance self-understanding and social trust.
