## 🎯 The Core of This Book
This book is a philosophical dialogue that interprets the ethical, political, and epistemological crises of contemporary society through the lens of "New Realism," proposed by マルクス・ガブリエル. Gabriel argues for the need to restore trust in "reality" in response to the "loss of reality" brought about by postmodern relativism.  

At the center of this discussion is the concept of the "crisis of representation." In an era where information and images obscure reality, there is a demand to restore ethics in the realms of democracy, capitalism, science, and technology, and to redefine human dignity.  

Gabriel presents a new vision of the world based on universal humanity through ethical capitalism and "politics of obvious facts." This is not merely a philosophical reflection but a practical proposal that reexamines the foundations of modern society.  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **The "Crisis of Representation" is the loss of reality**  
  In today's world, where social media and media prioritize "showing," fiction is beginning to overshadow reality. Gabriel refers to this situation as the "crisis of representation," emphasizing the need for philosophical thinking to restore the boundary between truth and falsehood.  

- **Democracy is a "mechanism to prevent violence"**  
  Democracy is not just about freedom of opinion; it is a system that mediates to prevent conflicting opinions from turning into violence. Gabriel advocates for politics based on "obvious facts" and aims to reconstruct its ethical foundation.  

- **The "lie" of capitalism and the lack of ethics**  
  The contemporary economy is supported by a "superiority of fiction," lacking ethical oversight. Proposing to place ethicists in companies and establish ethics committees is a practical measure to promote structural reform in capitalism.  

- **Technology makes humans mediocre**  
  AI and algorithms lead to "averages" rather than "best outcomes," posing a risk of homogenizing society and stifling creativity. Gabriel calls for the ethical use of technology centered on humanity.  

- **Scientism is a new superstition**  
  An absolutist attitude toward science shares the same structure as religious faith. Gabriel argues that unprincipled scientism destroys the Earth and humanity, making the integration of science and ethics essential.

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
The rapid evolution of generative AI has made Gabriel's "crisis of representation" a reality. In an era where AI substitutes human creativity and judgment, the meanings of "reality" and "humanity" are being redefined. New Realism is gaining attention as an ethical compass in this virtualized society.  

Moreover, phenomena such as historical revisionism and the hollowing out of culture are actualizing the metaphor of "the needle of world history being rewound." Movements for scientific verification and cultural reevaluation resonate with Gabriel's call for a "return to truth."  

Reaffirming universal humanity in a multicultural and multilingual environment aligns with the core of this book. The attempt to explore a "common ethics" that transcends relativism is a pressing issue in today's divided society.  

---

## 🚀 Practical Suggestions
1. **Reexamine the premises of information**  
   By cultivating the habit of considering the premises on which information is created, rather than just its truthfulness, one can develop the ability to discern reality without being misled by fiction.  

2. **Establish ethics committees in companies and organizations**  
   By incorporating ethical judgment into economic activities, we can reduce the structural "lies" of capitalism and lay the groundwork for a sustainable society.  

3. **Redefine the relationship with technology**  
   It is necessary to reassess automation and AI not as "myths of efficiency" but as tools that support human creativity and ethics.  

4. **Support democracy based on "obvious facts"**  
   Sharing universal values such as human rights and environmental protection as "facts," beyond political positions, is the first step to preventing division.  

5. **Reconstruct education as a network of social ethics**  
   By reevaluating education not just as knowledge transmission but as a social mechanism to reduce errors, we can cultivate a culture that supports truth and ethics.

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『世界史の針が巻き戻るとき』 is a philosophical challenge that sharply illuminates the ethical and epistemological crises faced by modern civilization and suggests an exit through the thought of "New Realism." Gabriel's arguments, while abstract, are practical in their direct connection to real-world issues such as AI, capitalism, and democracy.
