## 🎯 The Core of This Book
This book sharply denounces the reality that biotechnologies such as gene editing and prenatal diagnosis are reproducing eugenic ideologies in new forms. The authors critically depict how advancements in science are redefining who is considered "normal" and who is "abnormal" in society under the pretext of creating "better humans."

Additionally, it reveals the structure of "invisibility," where disabled individuals are symbolically treated in discussions but not imagined as real human beings. Through their life with their daughter Laura, who has Down syndrome, the authors reconstruct the ethics of technology as a personal narrative, presenting an ethics of imagination and empathy.

The core question of this book is, "Who is included in the future?" It challenges readers to decipher the values hidden within the narratives of science and confronts them with the responsibility of shaping the future.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Science is Sold as a Narrative**  
  Scientific technology is marketed not as neutral knowledge but as a "story that brings benefits." The authors argue that critical literacy to decode this narrative structure is essential for understanding science.

- **The Invisibility of Disability Distorts Ethics**  
  Disabled individuals are often treated as "risks" or "exceptions" in discussions and are not imagined as actual beings. This invisibility narrows the ethical judgments of society.

- **The Danger of Market-Driven Ethics**  
  The authors criticize the reality where discussions of bioethics are replaced by market principles, and consumer choices function as "ethical decisions." There is a need to reclaim ethics as a matter of public deliberation.

- **"Abnormal" is a Social Construct**  
  Classifications of "abnormal" at the genetic level disguise social discrimination as scientific objectivity. The authors expose the political nature of the binary of "normal/abnormal."

- **The Ethics of Imagination in Shaping the Future**  
  As the adage suggests, "Those who fail to imagine today will not exist in the future," the issue of who to include in the future is the fundamental ethical challenge of the technological age.

---

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
The issues raised in this book are increasingly urgent in today's world, where AI-driven genetic analysis and the proliferation of NIPT are advancing. When technology is wrapped in terms like "health," "optimization," and "risk management," the eugenic values lurking behind become less visible.

Moreover, the current lack of representation of disabled individuals in media and advertising exposes the limits of societal imagination. The authors' warning attempts to redefine technology ethics not as an issue for experts but as a matter of civic imagination and responsibility.

In contemporary society, the question of "Who is included in the future?" confronts all of us living in the age of AI, genetics, and data.

---

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Read Scientific Narratives Critically**  
   Consciously decode the values and exclusions hidden behind narratives that emphasize the benefits of technology. This is the first step for citizens to develop ethical judgment.

2. **Incorporate Disabled Voices into Social Discussions**  
   Establish mechanisms to visualize the narratives of disabled individuals in policies and media, allowing for a more equitable design of technological directions.

3. **Create Spaces for Deliberation Against Market-Driven Ethics**  
   It is necessary to cultivate a culture that examines the merits of science and technology through public dialogue rather than consumer behavior.

4. **Be Aware of the Power of Metaphors**  
   Continuously examine how metaphors like "DNA = blueprint" and "genes = destiny" shape reality, and maintain ethical awareness in the use of language.

5. **Continuously Question Who to Include in the Future**  
   In all areas of education, policy, and media, make a persistent effort to include socially invisible individuals in our imaginations as a first step towards a society of coexistence.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『あなたが消された未来』 is a rare book that illustrates the ethical blind spots surrounding advancements in science and technology through the intersection of personal narratives and social structures. It offers not just a critique of technology but introduces a new perspective on the ethics of imagination.

In a modern society that venerates technology, it emphasizes the importance of cultivating the ability to read science as a "narrative." This book prompts deep reflection for readers who wish to reconsider the boundaries between ethics, science, and society.
