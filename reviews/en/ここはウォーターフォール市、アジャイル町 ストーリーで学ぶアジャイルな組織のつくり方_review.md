## 🎯 Core of the Book
This book is a practical story that depicts the transformation from a rigid waterfall organizational culture to a flexible and learning-oriented agile organization.  
Set in the fictional "Hamana Precision Co., Ltd.," a typical vertically structured and plan-driven company, the narrative carefully illustrates the characters' journey as they experiment and gradually embrace agile values.

The authors present agile not merely as a development methodology, but as an "attitude of adapting to change." Through specific practices such as ticket management, daily stand-ups, retrospectives, and a culture of appreciation, they outline a path to balance organizational learning and psychological safety.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Agile is an "attitude of embracing change."**  
  The book's primary message is to view agile not just as a methodology but as a culture. The ability to shift from A to B, rather than choosing between A or B, is crucial, with a mindset that assumes change fostering organizational flexibility.

- **"Visibility" and "synchronization" support the team.**  
  Ticket management and daily stand-ups serve as mechanisms for sharing tasks and keeping the entire team informed. This reduces individual dependency and naturally fosters a culture of mutual support.

- **Small experiments lead to significant changes.**  
  It is more important to repeat small trials in short cycles than to aim for a perfect plan. An attitude that transforms failures into learning experiences sustains organizational growth.

- **Retrospectives solidify learning.**  
  Regular retrospectives using methods like KPT and YWT enhance team maturity. Reflecting on actions and continuously improving is at the core of agile culture.

- **Gratitude and appreciation foster psychological safety.**  
  By visualizing gratitude through mechanisms like "appreciation walls" and "thank you boards," members' actions are highlighted, creating an environment where they can challenge themselves safely.

---

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
In recent years, organizations across both public and private sectors have increasingly faced complex, ambiguous challenges. Amid changes such as population decline, digital transformation (DX), and diverse work styles, traditional waterfall planning often falls short.

The "test small and improve" mindset depicted in this book resonates with these societal trends. Particularly, agile thinking is becoming essential in local governments and public institutions, where resident participation and transparent decision-making are increasingly emphasized.

Moreover, the essence of agile—"psychological safety," "cross-boundary collaboration," and "co-creation"—aligns with key concepts in modern organizational development and talent cultivation. Thus, this book serves not only as a guide for improving development environments but also as a framework for organizational building in an era of change.

---

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Implement ticket management and daily stand-ups.**  
   Establish a system for sharing tasks and tracking progress as a team. This reduces individual dependency and cultivates a culture of mutual support.

2. **Repeat small experiments in short cycles.**  
   Rather than seeking perfection, conduct trials over short periods, such as weekly. Small successes build team confidence and drive transformation.

3. **Make regular retrospectives a team culture.**  
   Utilize KPT and YWT to formalize learning. By institutionalizing retrospectives, create an organization where improvement happens naturally.

4. **Create a system to visualize gratitude and appreciation.**  
   Introduce thank you boards or similar tools to facilitate members expressing gratitude to one another. This enhances psychological safety and encourages challenges.

5. **Advance change with a "best of both worlds" mindset.**  
   Avoid binary thinking; flexibly combine existing systems with new methods. Practical knowledge for advancing realistic change lies here.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『ここはウォーターフォール市、アジャイル町』 is a rare practical book that portrays agile as a "story of human and organizational growth." By conveying the real struggles and learnings in the field beyond theories and methodologies, it instills a sense of "we can change too" in readers.

In particular, the perspective of reinterpreting agile as an "attitude of continuously adapting" offers insights for everyone involved in digital transformation and organizational development. In a rapidly changing era, this book serves as a practical compass for those aspiring to create "learning organizations."
