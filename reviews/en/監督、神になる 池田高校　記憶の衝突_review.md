## 🎯 The Core of This Book
This book is a nonfiction work that reexamines Tsuta Fumiya, the coach of Ikeda High School, which won the Koshien championship three times, not merely as a legendary figure or a target of accusations, but through the conflicting memories of those involved. It presents both his dedicated efforts in sewing baseballs and maintaining the field, as well as his later distance from the scene, as part of the same individual's history.

Ikeda's success was not solely due to Tsuta's coaching ability, but also resulted from the support of players, coaches, the community, funding, metal bats, strength training, and a series of coincidences. The author suggests that understanding a person does not mean eliminating contradictions, but rather continuing to question while holding onto inconsistent testimonies.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Heroes are created from achievements and others' expectations**  
  The fact that "Ikeda won" led the media and the public to read into Tsuta's exceptional philosophy and form a myth. When significant achievements are attributed to a single individual's ability, collaboration, environment, and chance become obscured.

- **Organizations need to be restructured after success**  
  As the number of team members grew from eleven to fifty, and the work involving lectures, dormitories, donations, and expeditions expanded, Tsuta became unable to see each individual. Growth is both a testament to success and a factor that can disrupt the previously individual-dependent management.

- **Trust resides in daily actions rather than words**  
  The initial members' trust in Tsuta was based more on his actions—repairing worn-out balls and quietly maintaining the field—than on grand speeches. For trust in a leader, concrete interactions that make one feel "seen" are essential.

- **Leaving contradictions can lead to a better understanding of humanity**  
  The testimonies of someone who invested personal wealth and someone who was obsessed with money cannot be easily reconciled. This book does not exclude either perspective; instead, it juxtaposes different images of Tsuta based on time and position, freeing humanity from a binary good-and-evil framework.

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
In recent years, scrutiny of high school baseball has increased, with a stronger focus on examining the narratives of renowned coaches and prestigious schools, including issues like corporal punishment, organizational governance, funding, and community pressure. This book aligns with that trend, not condemning the past solely by contemporary values, but also considering the conditions under which the memories of those involved were formed.

Moreover, the competitive environment for public schools has significantly changed due to the outflow of players to other prefectures and the diversification of school choices. The book's stance of not attributing Ikeda's rise and fall solely to one coach's abilities is also applicable to discussions about "charismatic dependency" in sports, businesses, and community organizations.

---

## 🚀 Practical Suggestions
1. **Break down success factors into multiple components**  
   Avoid attributing achievements solely to the leader's talent; instead, list out factors such as personnel, collaborators, funding, tools, systems, timing, and chance. By avoiding heroification, one can distinguish between replicable innovations and unrepeatable luck.

2. **Redesign roles in accordance with organizational growth**  
   As the number of members and tasks increases, clarify who will oversee each member and who will connect the front lines with the top. Continuing with pre-success management alone can lead to overwork for leaders and isolation for the team.

3. **Demonstrate interest through visible actions**  
   Engage with the field, maintain shared resources, and reach out to individuals about their changes. Sustaining actions that convey "I am watching" is crucial; merely stating ideals cannot fill the void of neglect or alienation felt by others.

4. **Explain the background of decision-making to stakeholders**  
   Share the purposes and constraints regarding external activities, fundraising, and personnel with those affected. Gaps in explanation can lead to speculation, and even good intentions and necessary actions can be perceived as betrayals.

5. **Do not rush to integrate conflicting testimonies**  
   Record the testimonies separately based on the speakers' positions, timing, observed facts, and emotions. Rather than eliminating contradictions as flaws, treating them as traces of change in individuals or organizations leads to a more accurate understanding.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
The significance of this book lies not in dragging Tsuta Fumiya down from the pedestal of charisma, but in reclaiming the complexity of "a person made into a god." By vividly depicting the simultaneous existence of admiration and disappointment, dedication and mundanity, innovation and chance, it illustrates how success stories are constructed and how those narratives can constrain both the individual and their surroundings.

This book is suitable for readers interested in the history of high school baseball, as well as for leaders, educators, managers, and those contemplating the growth of organizations and community. Rather than providing a refreshing conclusion, it offers a reading experience that lingers in the clash of memories, allowing one to savor the difficulty and sincerity of understanding others.
