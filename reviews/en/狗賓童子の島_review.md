## 🎯 The Core of This Book
『狗賓童子の島』 is a historical literature that depicts the turbulent era of the Bakumatsu and Meiji Restoration from the peripheral perspective of Oki, rather than the central power. Kazukazu Iijima presents the themes of "the dignity of the people" and "the power of self-reliance" through the portrayal of the populace finding ways to live on their own and resisting the structures of domination.

At the heart of this book lies a cold insight into the corruption of power and its continuity. Even as the regime shifts from the shogunate to the new government, the structure that forces the populace to bear the brunt of sacrifice remains unchanged. Iijima captures this reality through the actions of people who live not by ideals, but by deeds.

Moreover, through the "practices of knowledge and skills" such as medicine, agriculture, and coexistence with nature, the book questions how individuals can achieve self-reliance. The characters, who are aware of impermanence yet fulfill their responsibilities, encourage readers to adopt a deep sense of resolve.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **The Deception of the "Restoration" and the Reality of the People**  
  The "Restoration" is depicted not as liberation for the people, but as a deception that lies on the extension of the old regime. This perspective that the essence of the power structure does not change provides an opportunity to reconsider history from the viewpoint of the populace rather than that of the rulers.

- **Knowledge Holds Meaning Only Through Practice**  
  Symbolized by Jotaro's medical training, knowledge is not theoretical but becomes a force to save people only through action. The true understanding gained only through experience resonates as a lesson applicable to modern society.

- **The Possibility of Self-Governance and Self-Reliance of the People**  
  The attempts at self-governance by the people of Oki demonstrate that order can be maintained without central control. This proves the resilience of the populace in living independently from authority.

- **The Ethics of Living in Harmony with Nature**  
  Characters who resist a monetary economy and live based on the blessings of nature serve as a warning to the modern world engulfed in materialism. The harmony with nature is depicted as a fundamental force that supports human existence.

- **Justice as Action Rather than Ideals**  
  As symbolized by the phrase "Ideals cannot replace food," it is the actions that sustain others in reality, rather than abstract ideals, that constitute true justice. The difficulty and nobility of practicing ethics come to the forefront.

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
In modern Japan, the centralization of political structures and the widening economic disparities raise questions about the autonomy of regions and individuals. The "self-governance of the people" and "living in harmony with nature" depicted in 『狗賓童子の島』 present an alternative model of life in these times.

Furthermore, in an era of globalization and information overload, where knowledge is often divorced from practice and ideals become hollow, the assertion in this book that "knowledge holds meaning only through action" carries sharp critical significance. It prompts a reevaluation of the value of knowledge rooted in human "hands" and "hearts" in a society dominated by AI and efficiency.

Additionally, in a contemporary society facing environmental crises and increasing isolation, themes of coexistence with nature and the reconstruction of communities can be read not merely as nostalgia but as guidance for the future. Iijima's writing, while depicting the past, questions modern ethics and ways of living.

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Choose Action Over Ideals**  
   Rather than merely advocating for ideals, take actions to help those around you. Small, reality-based practices can be the first step toward changing society.

2. **Test Knowledge in the Field**  
   Apply what you have learned in practice, accumulating experience without fear of failure. As Jotaro's attitude shows, true understanding is gained only through practice.

3. **Be Conscious of Coexistence with Nature**  
   Reintroduce the rhythms of nature into your daily life. Self-sufficient innovations and utilizing local resources can serve as resistance against materialism.

4. **Make Your Own Judgments Without Relying on Authority**  
   Do not blindly follow the directives of society or organizations; make decisions based on your values and experiences. Incorporating the spirit of self-governance into personal life is the path to independence.

5. **Accept Impermanence and Live in the Present**  
   Be aware of the finiteness of life and act with full effort on what you can do now, without procrastination. Awareness of impermanence can become a driving force for action.

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『狗賓童子の島』 is a profound and universal epic that portrays human dignity and self-reliance from the periphery of history. Kazukazu Iijima's writing transcends mere historical reproduction, illuminating the reality of the populace's lives and ethical strength.

This work excavates the "untold history" buried within the central narrative, prompting a reevaluation of the meaning of self-reliance and coexistence in contemporary society. By depicting the nobility of living through action rather than ideals, this book offers quiet resolve and hope to all readers standing at the crossroads of change.
