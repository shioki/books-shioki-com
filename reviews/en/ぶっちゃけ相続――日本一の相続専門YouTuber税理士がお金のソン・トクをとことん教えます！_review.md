## 🎯 The Core of This Book
The core of this book lies in its thorough exploration of "knowledge and practices to prevent inheritance from becoming a 'family dispute'." It presents methods for achieving harmonious inheritance from three perspectives: law, taxation, and psychology, all explained in an easily understandable manner from the viewpoint of an expert.

The author, 橘慶太, emphasizes real-world practices as a practitioner who has dealt with numerous consultation cases on YouTube, focusing not only on theory but also on practical realities. He breaks down themes such as statutory inheritance shares, will creation, lifetime gifts, and responses to tax audits, allowing readers to understand them as personal issues.

Ultimately, the book concludes that "the trust within the family is the best tax-saving strategy." It redefines inheritance not merely as a distribution of assets but as an act of safeguarding the family's future, which is its most distinctive feature.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **"Statutory inheritance shares are just a guideline"—Flexible division is possible**  
  Inheritance is not bound by statutory ratios; it can be freely divided with the agreement of all parties. Understanding the legal framework and prioritizing consensus among family members is the first step to preventing disputes.

- **"Special benefits have no statute of limitations"—Importance of long-term gift management**  
  Past gifts are recalculated at the time of inheritance, making it essential to accurately record long-term asset transfers. A lack of records can lead to future disputes and tax risks.

- **"Inheritance tax decreases with more heirs"—Understanding the system structure is key to tax savings**  
  By understanding the tax system and designing with primary and secondary inheritances in mind, legal tax savings become possible. This requires a long-term asset design rather than one-off measures.

- **"Name deposits are the most scrutinized point"—Substance over form is questioned**  
  Simply changing names does not qualify as a gift; actual management authority is emphasized. How bankbooks and seals are handled can determine whether or not it is subject to taxation.

- **"Assessing the expertise of tax accountants"—Practical experience is more important than qualifications**  
  Tax accountants who specialize in inheritance tax are limited, making it crucial to assess them before hiring. It is recommended to select based on practical abilities such as explanation skills and investigative attitudes.

## 🔍 Relevance to Modern Context
As Japan's society ages, the number of inheritance cases is rapidly increasing. Since the reduction of the basic exemption in 2015, the scope of inheritance tax has expanded, making tax planning a realistic issue even for ordinary households.

Furthermore, new rules for gift taxes from 2024 and the reevaluation of real estate assessments in 2026 have made traditional tax-saving schemes less applicable. In this context, the book's advocacy for "early preparation," "family consensus building," and "collaboration with experts" is becoming increasingly important.

The author's emphasis on "family harmony as the best inheritance strategy" should be reevaluated not merely as an emotional argument but as a practical strategy in an era of institutional reform and tax risks.

---

## 🚀 Practical Suggestions
1. **Obtain a "capacity assessment certificate" when creating a will**  
   Attaching a doctor's certification ensures the validity of the will and prevents later claims of invalidity. This is the most practical means to enhance legal stability.

2. **Create a gift contract each time and retain handwritten signatures**  
   Clearly documenting the fact of the gift serves as evidence during tax audits. Consistency in dates and signatures enhances reliability beyond just formal documents.

3. **Check and store bank statements for the past 10 years**  
   This is the most critical material for tax audits and serves as the basis for determining the existence of name deposits. Requesting transaction histories from banks should be done promptly.

4. **Hold regular family meetings to share inheritance policies**  
   By discussing inheritance openly and early, emotional conflicts can be prevented. Building trust among family members is the most effective risk avoidance strategy.

5. **Compare and select multiple tax accountants who specialize in inheritance tax**  
   Meet with several experts before hiring, judging them based on their explanatory skills, track record, and understanding of secondary inheritances. Assessing expertise ultimately influences tax-saving effectiveness.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『ぶっちゃけ相続』 is a practical book that organizes the complex and emotional theme of inheritance through the integration of law, taxation, and psychology. The author's rich field experience and concrete examples eliminate the abstract discussions often found in specialized texts, transforming them into actionable knowledge for readers.

Particularly, the conclusion that "family harmony is the best tax-saving strategy" remains a universal truth even in today's era of institutional reform. This book provides an opportunity to rethink inheritance not just as a tax issue but as a design for the family's future.
