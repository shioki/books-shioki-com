## 🎯 The Core of This Book
『にほんでいきる』 is a report that meticulously portrays the realities of foreign children who have been regarded as "invisible" within Japanese society through thorough research. The authors reveal the structural issues that lead to children outside the compulsory education framework being deprived of educational opportunities and becoming isolated from society.

While highlighting the reality that not understanding Japanese can lead to academic delays, school refusal, and even delinquency or crime, the book presents the resilience of education through the stories of boys who have reconstructed their lives by learning the language.

Additionally, it introduces advanced initiatives from municipalities such as Kani City and Matsusaka City, empirically demonstrating that educational support enhances the sustainability of local communities. The overarching theme is the philosophy of "leaving no one behind" and the hope for building a society of coexistence.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **"Invisible Children" Created by Systemic Blind Spots**  
  The legal loophole that exempts foreign children from compulsory education has led to governmental indifference. The educational void is a structural issue that reproduces social isolation and poverty.

- **The Power of Language to Transform Lives**  
  The experiences of a boy who learned Japanese in a juvenile detention center symbolize that language education is the starting point for self-reconstruction. By acquiring language skills, children can finally connect with society and envision their futures.

- **Educational Support Transforms Communities**  
  As shown in the case of Kani City, educational support is not merely welfare but a social investment that enhances the sustainability of communities. Children who receive support become taxpayers, creating a cycle that supports the local society.

- **"Invisible Walls" Deepen Isolation**  
  Misunderstandings arising from language and cultural differences can drive children towards self-denial. The term "invisible language barrier" symbolizes the greatest obstacle in a society of coexistence.

- **A Coexisting Society Leads to Happiness for All**  
  The phrase "A city that is easy for foreigners to live in is also easy for Japanese people" encapsulates the core of this book. Achieving coexistence is not just for a specific minority but signifies the maturity of society as a whole.

---

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
The issues addressed in this book are increasingly urgent in modern Japan, where the number of foreign residents is rapidly rising. In places like Ibaraki Prefecture and Okayama Prefecture, efforts are being made to establish multicultural coexistence education and Japanese language support systems to respond to the growing number of foreign students. However, significant regional disparities still exist nationwide.

Moreover, with the child poverty rate remaining high in Japan, the educational disparities faced by foreign families contribute to social reproduction. The realities depicted in 『にほんでいきる』 visualize the challenges at the intersection of educational policy, community welfare, and labor policy, providing guidance for building a society of coexistence.

---

## 🚀 Proposals for Action
1. **Institutionalize School Support for Foreign Children**  
   It is necessary to establish a legal framework that guarantees the right to education regardless of nationality. Clarifying the system will encourage governmental responsibility and rectify educational inequalities.

2. **Provide Early and Systematic Japanese Language Education**  
   Delays in language acquisition impact overall learning, making support for Japanese language education from early childhood essential. Schools, communities, and volunteers should collaborate to build a continuous educational system.

3. **Expand Support Networks through Local Communities**  
   By developing systems that support the entire community starting from schools, as seen in Kani City, we can prevent the isolation of foreign families and enhance the vitality of local societies.

4. **Strengthen Multicultural Responsiveness in Educational Settings**  
   It is crucial for teachers and supporters to understand the differences in cultural backgrounds and adopt an approach that is attentive to each child's needs. Training in multicultural coexistence education and the development of teaching materials are required.

5. **Enhance Support for Advancement and Employment**  
   The high dropout rate among foreign high school students indicates a lack of institutional support. It is urgent to open pathways to social participation through flexible high school entrance systems and vocational training.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『にほんでいきる』 serves as a powerful reminder of the role of journalism in delivering the "voiceless voices" of foreign children to society. The reporting team not only highlights the systemic blind spots but also illustrates the possibilities of a coexistence society through the efforts and hopes of supporters.

This book should be read not only by educators and government officials but by everyone living in the local community. It challenges readers with the question, "Who are we leaving behind?" and possesses the power to provoke thought about the nature of a society where we live together.
