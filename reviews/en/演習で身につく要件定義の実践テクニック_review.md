## 🎯 The Core of This Book
This book redefines "requirements definition" in system development not merely as a preliminary process but as a core process that connects business reform and management improvement. The author, Tetsuro Mizuta, systematizes requirements definition based on his extensive practical experience in the field, focusing on three pillars: "logical thinking," "visualization," and "consensus building."

Notably, the approach of understanding problems through a three-layer structure of "system," "business," and "management" allows for the identification of fundamental organizational issues rather than just superficial system requests, enabling a coherent connection between business design and system requirements.

Additionally, the book provides detailed explanations of communication techniques such as interviewing and meeting design, presented in a format that practitioners can immediately apply. It serves as a practical guide for acquiring "usable requirements definition" in the field, oscillating between theory and practice.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Requirements definition begins with clarifying the "systemization policy."**  
  By documenting the policy at the start of development, it prevents later rework and unifies stakeholders' understanding. This is a crucial branching point that significantly affects the project's success rate.

- **Understanding problems through a three-layer structure is key to approaching the essence.**  
  By organizing issues at the levels of system, business, and management, one can identify root causes rather than merely responding to phenomena. This leads to true business reform through system implementation.

- **Logical organization using logic trees prevents omissions.**  
  By visualizing problems through causal relationships, a common understanding among the entire team can be formed. Structured thinking prevents ambiguous discussions and enhances the quality of decision-making.

- **Solutions should be conceived as a redesign of the entire business.**  
  Rather than focusing solely on systems, aim for comprehensive improvements that include systems, organizations, and workplace environments. This mindset achieves overall optimization that transcends partial optimization.

- **Interviews and facilitation influence outcomes.**  
  By employing questioning techniques and innovative meeting designs, one can accurately elicit opinions from stakeholders. Requirements definition is supported by the "ability to listen" and the "ability to create a conducive environment."

## 🔍 Relevance to Modern Context
In the current era of digital transformation (DX), the quality of requirements definition has become a key factor influencing a company's competitiveness. While the adoption of AI and cloud technologies accelerates, the risk of systems being misaligned with on-site operations is also increasing.

The "requirements definition that connects business and management" presented in this book serves as a practical prescription for these challenges. It provides essential guidance for modern project managers and business analysts as a way of thinking that goes beyond mere technical implementation to redesigning the organization's objectives and structure.

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Create a "systemization policy document" in the initial stages.**  
   Clearly articulate the purpose, scope, and expected outcomes, and share it with management to prevent later discrepancies in understanding. It serves as a "compass" that defines the project's direction.

2. **Use logic trees to structure problems.**  
   Organize current issues by causal relationships and visualize them in a way that third parties can understand. This promotes transparency in discussions and consensus building.

3. **Share objectives and questions before interviews.**  
   Sending a preliminary request letter encourages the other party to prepare and enhances the quality of dialogue. This leads to efficient information gathering and relationship building.

4. **Incorporate "physical environment" into meeting design.**  
   The layout, such as a V-shape or the arrangement of whiteboards, can influence the productivity of discussions. Evolve meetings from "information-sharing sessions" to "consensus-building sessions."

5. **Consider five components when discussing solutions.**  
   By examining improvement measures from the perspectives of business processes, systems and rules, organization and structure, information systems, and workplace environments, overall optimization can be achieved.

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『演習で身につく要件定義の実践テクニック』 is a practical book that reconstructs requirements definition as an "intellectual design activity that connects people and organizations." It is not just a procedural manual but provides a comprehensive skill set that integrates logical thinking, visualization, and communication.

Especially for SEs, consultants, and project managers struggling with requirements definition in the field, this book offers practical guidance and reproducible methods. Its structure, oscillating between theory and practice, empowers readers to internalize knowledge as "usable" rather than merely "understanding it."
