## 🎯 The Core of This Book
This book proposes a way of living that reduces anxiety by organizing "one's own world" without getting caught up in the speed of society or the expectations of others. The author, 大原扁理, has learned through ten years of retirement life that it is more effective to "observe and let go" of anxiety rather than trying to eliminate it.

By perceiving anxiety as "temporary weather" and maintaining a stance that avoids getting caught up in others' subjective worlds, the book shows a path to regain inner peace. The philosophy that living as a "self-respecting self" is more important than social success or external validation leads to true freedom and happiness.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Anxiety is a "state" and not a "personality"**  
  The perspective that treats anxiety as a temporary "weather disturbance" rather than a permanent flaw is presented. This mindset allows for a natural recovery without falling into self-denial.

- **Avoid getting caught up in others' subjective worlds**  
  The assertion that one does not need to follow the value systems created by others is particularly sharp in today's era of social media. Organizing one's own world on a smaller scale serves as a mental defense.

- **Honesty in not saying "what you don't mean"**  
  Restraining from casual "likes!" or social niceties leads to self-consistency and the restoration of trust. Not ignoring small feelings of discomfort supports long-term stability.

- **Being a "self-respecting self"**  
  The focus shifts from others' evaluations to whether one can take pride in their own actions. This internal standard fosters an independent way of living that is not swayed by anxiety.

- **Responsibility not to transmit anxiety to society**  
  Individual stability is directly linked to the overall health of society. The insight that the more people can manage their own anxiety, the calmer the social atmosphere becomes, is striking.

---

## 🔍 Connection to Contemporary Context
The ideas in this book resonate deeply with the post-COVID trend of "living small" and "stepping back from competition." In the face of increasing societal pressures such as long working hours and rising prices, the author's proposals sound like a practical prescription for "living within one's means."

Moreover, the attitude of seeking inner peace while accepting the impermanence of society, akin to 鴨長明's 『方丈記』, can be seen as a mental survival skill in today's anxious society. By presenting a "quiet and sincere way of living" beyond mere lifestyle techniques, the book possesses a depth that elevates it to a philosophical work.

---

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Don't say "what you don't mean"**  
   Practice aligning your emotions with your words in small daily situations. This will help restore self-trust and make you less susceptible to others' influences.

2. **Intentionally create "blank time"**  
   Step away from smartphones and news to have moments of doing nothing, which can help relieve mental congestion. This is the best reset method in an age of information overload.

3. **Treat anxiety as "weather"**  
   When feeling anxious, recognize it as "cloudy" and avoid forcing it to clear up. It's important to trust that time will naturally lead to recovery.

4. **Be aware of boundaries with others' worlds**  
   Adjust your distance from social media and news to protect your subjective world. This is also an act of enhancing your mental immunity.

5. **Make "self-respecting choices"**  
   When in doubt, ask yourself, "Does this action allow me to respect myself?" The accumulation of small acts of honesty brings long-term stability.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『隠居生活10年目 不安は９割捨てました』 serves as a quiet rebuttal to the "anxious society" of today. The author redefines the distance from society with an attitude of "organizing" rather than escaping.

This book is a practical philosophical guide for those who are exhausted from overexertion or constrained by others' evaluations, offering a way to lighten the heart. After reading, one is left with a new sense of how to "live quietly with anxiety" rather than simply trying to eliminate it.
