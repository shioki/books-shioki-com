## 🎯 Core of the Book
『夏が破れる』 is a work that depicts the structure of exploitation and violence lurking behind economic development through the lens of individual trauma. From the very beginning, reminiscent of human trafficking incidents in Thailand, readers are dragged into the harsh reality where human dignity is trampled upon.

Through the perspective of the protagonist, Shin, the narrative illustrates the collapse of boundaries between perpetration and victimization, justice and corruption, reality and nightmare. The memories of violence replay across time, and the erosion of humanity in the name of systems and beliefs symbolizes the ethical crisis of contemporary society.

Yet, beneath the story lies a faint light, represented by interactions with animals and small acts of kindness from others. 新庄耕 gazes into the darkness of humanity while never relinquishing the possibility of redemption.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Violence Repeats in Different Forms**  
  The structure where Shin, a victim of bullying, later gets caught up in violence illustrates the reality that society cannot break the cycle of violence. The silence imposed on victims allows the perpetrator structure to persist.

- **Systems Do Not Guarantee Justice**  
  Scenes where the police side with the perpetrators symbolize the corruption of institutions and the collapse of ethics. The fear that the mechanisms meant to uphold justice become accomplices to violence instills deep distrust in readers.

- **Human Cruelty Lurks in Everyday Life**  
  The depiction of pig farming as a cover for violence shows that cruelty is not an anomaly but an extension of the everyday. Violence is not a distant occurrence but something that lurks within our lives.

- **Salvation Resides in Small Acts of Kindness**  
  As the saying goes, "We help each other in times of trouble," selfless compassion becomes the key to breaking the cycle of violence. This light that pierces through despair is what enables human regeneration.

- **The Mixing of Faith and Science Breeds Madness**  
  The ideology of Shinsuke, who reveres the pineal gland as the "third eye," depicts a precarious fusion of reason and fanaticism. It shows that when knowledge intertwines with faith, it can destroy humanity in the most terrifying ways.

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
The "cycle of violence and isolation" depicted in this book resonates deeply with the realities faced by Japanese society in the 2020s. With extreme heat and climate change eroding people's mental and physical well-being, and the decline of rural areas alongside the isolation of youth, social violence is being reborn in different forms.

Recent studies have revealed that climate stress has a severe impact on mental health. The "collapse of summer" portrayed by 新庄耕 is not merely a metaphor for the season but symbolizes the spiritual exhaustion of modern Japan.

In an era of increasing social media dependency and institutional distrust, the importance of regaining small acts of kindness and ethical judgment towards others is being questioned anew through this work.

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Create Spaces to Speak About Memories of Violence**  
   Silence becomes a breeding ground for violence. Sharing past pain in a safe environment can be the first step toward recovery and solidarity.

2. **Make Ethical Judgments Independently of Institutions**  
   It is necessary to confront the reality that institutions do not necessarily guarantee justice and to cultivate individual ethical sensitivity.

3. **Accumulate Small Acts of Kindness in Daily Life**  
   The attitude of "We help each other in times of trouble" can alleviate social violence. If selfless compassion becomes a chain reaction, it can resist structural violence.

4. **Take Bodily Changes as Warnings from the Mind**  
   Changes in bodily sensations are signs alerting us to mental crises. Being sensitive to the voice of the mind and body is key to preventing collapse.

5. **Stop Being a Bystander and Raise Your Voice Against Abnormalities**  
   "Turning a blind eye" is complicity in violence. When you notice small injustices, having the courage to act rather than remain silent can change society.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『夏が破れる』 is an extremely profound literary work that illuminates the distortions of contemporary society through the lens of violence and the collapse of ethics. 新庄耕 portrays the figure of humanity swaying between trauma and redemption with a cold, precise pen.

Through this story, readers will not only confront the structural violence of society but also face the "complicity of silence" that lurks within themselves. When they discover the faint hope that glimmers amidst despair, they will surely sense the possibility of regeneration that lies beyond the "broken summer."
