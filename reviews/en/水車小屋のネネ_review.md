## 🎯 The Core of This Book
『水車小屋のネネ』 is a story about sisters living in poverty and family breakdown, who achieve independence through the goodwill and labor of others. The protagonists, Risa and Ritsu, embody the truth of life that "independence is not isolation" as they support each other.  

The narrative illustrates how goodwill can create a chain reaction and shape character through their relationships with those who support them. Symbolic figures like the watermill and the parrot Nene quietly underpin the story of memory, time, forgiveness, and rebirth.  

Tsukimura Kikuko vividly brings to light the meaning of human dignity and coexistence with others through realistic depictions of labor and life.  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Independence is Established Through Mutual Support**  
  Risa perceives "independence" not as rejecting help from others, but as a choice accompanied by gratitude and responsibility. Independence is portrayed as a form of maturity based on relationships, not isolation.  

- **The Chain of Goodwill Shapes Character**  
  As Ritsu expresses, "I am made of others' conscience," individuals are shaped by the cumulative actions of others. Goodwill is presented not as a transient act, but as the foundation of character.  

- **Intelligence and Pride Exist Even in Poverty**  
  Tsukimura depicts "poverty" not as a human weakness, but as fertile ground for understanding and empathy, emphasizing the dignity that comes from working earnestly without shame over economic difficulties.  

- **Working is Life Itself**  
  Labor is not merely a means of livelihood, but a connection to the world and an act of affirming one's existence. Risa's approach to work embodies the fundamental will to live.  

- **From Being Supported to Supporting Others**  
  Just as Ritsu guides the next generation, the chain of support continues across generations. People are helped by someone and eventually transform into those who help others.  

---

## 🔍 Connection to Contemporary Context
Modern society is characterized by increasing individualism and isolation, while there is a demand for the reconstruction of mutual aid and trust. The "community of support" depicted in this work can be seen as a response to this contemporary need.  

As noted by Miyake Kaho, the phrase "I want to celebrate the fact that such a story has emerged in this harsh era" is symbolic. Tsukimura's writing reaffirms the beauty of trusting others amidst the anxieties and disconnections of modern life.  

The watermill, as a symbol of community, can be read as a parable that encourages a redefinition of "living together" in a world fragmented by social media and economic disparities.

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Have the Courage to Accept Help**  
   Independence begins not with rejecting others, but with mutual trust. Seeking support in difficult times is a step toward maturity.  

2. **Be Mindful of Small Acts of Kindness in Daily Life**  
   Like Professor Fujisawa's words, being kind to someone enriches life. The accumulation of small acts can rebuild trust in society.  

3. **View Work as Self-Expression**  
   Labor is not just a means to live; it is an act of affirming one's value. Approaching any job with sincerity can create a foundation for life.  

4. **Have a Place of Memory**  
   Like the watermill, having a place where you can recall your past and connections with others is essential. It becomes a base for the regeneration of the heart.  

5. **Pass On the Goodness You Received**  
   Cultivate the awareness of returning the goodwill you have received to others. The chain of support generates sustainable hope for society.  

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『水車小屋のネネ』 is a long novel that deeply explores the theme of "sustaining relationships between people" within Tsukimura Kikuko's body of work. The forty years depicted in the quiet prose of the sisters and those around them challenge the meaning of "living together" beyond the divisions of modern society.  

Readers will sense the warmth and power of regeneration that can only be gained through relationships with others. This story offers quiet courage to those weary of life, feeling isolated, and anyone wishing to support someone else.
