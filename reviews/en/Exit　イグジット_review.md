## 🎯 The Core of This Book
『Exit イグジット』 is a socio-economic novel that sharply exposes the reality of the Japanese economy distorted by ultra-low interest rate policies from a journalist's perspective. It depicts the process in which the fundamental concept of interest rates disappears, leading to the collapse of ethics in banks and corporations, and the spread of moral hazard throughout society.

The story intertwines multiple layers, including finance, politics, media, and local economies, tracing how the nation's invisible asset of "credibility" is lost. Set against the backdrop of a Japanese society trapped in policy management with no exit—"No Exit"—it illustrates a structural crisis intertwined with institutional fatigue and human weakness.

Ultimately, the question posed is not about systems or policies, but about how far individual humans can maintain their conscience, presenting an ethical dilemma. While it is an economic novel, it can also be read as a human drama questioning the foundational elements of society: "trust" and "integrity."

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Low interest rate policies corrode ethics**  
  Monetary policy transcends economic manipulation, affecting human behavioral principles and social morals. The warning is clear: in a society where interest rates have disappeared, discipline and integrity are simultaneously lost.

- **Organizations that resist change will self-destruct**  
  Through depictions of the publishing industry and financial institutions, the precariousness of Japan's organizational culture, which fears change, comes to light. Ignoring environmental changes and clinging to outdated structures ultimately leads to self-destruction.

- **Loss of credibility can trigger national collapse**  
  The assertion that "credibility is invisible, but losing it will lead to the collapse of the nation" highlights the importance of trust, which underpins the economy. Maintaining values that cannot be measured by numbers is essential for the sustainability of society.

- **The gap between journalism ideals and reality**  
  The protagonist, a journalist, faces a dual reality: the mission to pursue truth and the powerlessness of reporting. In an era of information overload, fundamental questions arise about what to delve into and what to communicate.

- **The fear of policy management without an exit**  
  The term "No Exit" symbolizes a structural crisis from which there is no escape. The cumulative side effects of monetary easing are depicted as society slowly collapses without anyone taking responsibility.

## 🔍 Connection to Contemporary Context
In recent years, the term "EXIT" has frequently been used in the startup and investment world in Japan. The focus on exit strategies through M&A and IPOs has garnered attention, with the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry promoting multi-faceted exits as part of growth strategies. However, the "exit" depicted in this book is not about institutional or market exits, but rather about a society that has lost its way due to a lack of ethics and trust.

This contrast is symbolic. Current economic policies also continue to rely on "life-prolonging measures" such as low interest rates and monetary easing, falling into an inescapable structure. 相場英雄's depiction of "No Exit" seems to foresee the fundamental stagnation faced by contemporary Japanese economy and society.

Thus, this book can be read not merely as an economic novel but as a work that reexamines the ethical aspects of "exit strategies" in modern policy management and corporate governance.

## 🚀 Practical Suggestions
1. **Consider economic policies alongside ethics**  
   Discussions about monetary easing and interest rate manipulation must incorporate ethical and social impacts. The value of "trust," which cannot be measured by numbers, should be acknowledged.

2. **Embrace change and promote structural reform**  
   Clinging to outdated systems signifies decline. Both organizations and individuals must adopt a flexible approach to self-reform, assuming environmental changes.

3. **Confront the realities of local economies**  
   The side effects of policies emerge from local contexts. It is crucial to understand the realities of regional financial institutions and small businesses, reflecting their voices in policy formation.

4. **Take actions to protect credibility**  
   Trust, once lost, is hard to regain. Emphasizing transparency, integrity, and accountability is essential, prioritizing long-term trust over short-term gains.

5. **Reassess the ethics of reporting and information dissemination**  
   Avoid superficial reporting and strive to delve into the roots of structural issues. As information consumers, critical thinking is required to discern what is true.

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『Exit イグジット』 serves as a mirror of society, depicting the ethical crisis of contemporary Japan beyond the confines of an economic novel. The author’s ability to concretize the abstract theme of monetary policy into a narrative about human desires and conscience is remarkable, showcasing 相場英雄's keen observational skills.

By illustrating the human weaknesses lurking behind the institutions of economy, politics, and media, readers are prompted to question their own positions in a "society without exit." This book is not only for those interested in economics and media but also for anyone wishing to reflect on the ethical collapse of modern society, offering profound insights.
