## 🎯 Core of the Book
"Before DX" redefines the role of internal system engineers (SEs) not merely as system administrators but as "transformers for improving the company." The author, 綴間チヨノリ, emphasizes the importance of "thinking time" and "systematization" that are necessary before the stage of digital transformation (DX).  

At the heart of this book is the attitude of creating "space for thinking" through operational efficiency and automation, which allows for a deeper understanding of the essence of management issues. It outlines a pathway for internal SEs to become strategic entities that connect management with the field, addressing practical themes such as distinguishing between temporary and permanent solutions, eliminating dependency on specific individuals, and thinking in terms of overall optimization.  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **"Thinking time" is the greatest asset**  
  Even amidst the daily grind, securing time for thought is the most valuable investment. Without thinking time, improvements and strategies cannot emerge, and organizational growth will stagnate.  

- **Two-tier thinking of temporary and permanent solutions**  
  Mature responses involve not just temporary fixes during problem occurrences but also designing mechanisms to prevent recurrence. This perspective, which balances short-term stability with long-term improvement, serves as a benchmark for the capabilities of internal SEs.  

- **The power of "systematization" to prevent dependency**  
  Creating a business structure that anyone can replicate through manualization and automation supports organizational sustainability. Systematization is not merely about efficiency; it is the foundation for focusing on creative work.  

- **IT strategy is about "reducing unnecessary battles"**  
  The essence of strategy lies in discerning where to concentrate limited resources rather than merely introducing new technologies. The courage to decide what not to do leads to true overall optimization.  

- **The courage to choose challenges fosters transformation**  
  Choosing not to maintain the status quo and deliberately opting for difficult paths propels the company forward. Internal SEs are those who, unafraid of change, shape the future of the organization through challenges.  

---

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
The assertions in this book hold significant contemporary relevance by focusing on the "pre-stage" of promoting DX. In recent years, while the introduction of AI and automation technologies has advanced, issues such as dependency on specific individuals and ambiguity in problem definition have been pointed out as factors leading to DX failures. "Before DX" returns to this "Before" stage, advocating for the establishment of systems and time as the foundation for transformation.  

Moreover, in a society facing declining birth rates and labor shortages, the mindset of "solving through systems" is sought over simply "increasing personnel." The author's proposals present a universal approach to addressing such societal challenges.  

---

## 🚀 Suggestions for Practice
1. **Systematize and externalize routine tasks to secure "thinking time"**  
   Automate and outsource daily operations to create time for improvement and strategy formulation. This is the first step toward transformation.  

2. **Clearly distinguish between "temporary" and "permanent" responses when problems arise**  
   Establish a system that prevents being consumed by the same issues by not stopping at temporary fixes but implementing measures to prevent recurrence.  

3. **Prepare manuals at a level that "anyone can do"**  
   Prevent dependency and enhance the reproducibility of operations. By organizing images and procedures chronologically, reduce training costs and support stable organizational operations.  

4. **Prioritize troubles based on "whether business will stop"**  
   Make decisions based on business impact rather than technical difficulty, enabling decision-making with a management perspective.  

5. **Propose from the IT standpoint to the management**  
   Engage not just as a technical support but as a partner in solving management issues. This evolution allows internal SEs to play a significant role in management.  

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
"Before DX" is a rare practical book that deliberately focuses on the "pre-stage" in an era where the term DX is rampant. The author portrays internal SEs as "strategic entities that create systems and time," redefining their role.  

This book offers insights not only to IT departments but to all business professionals involved in operational improvement and organizational transformation. After reading, fundamental questions such as "How do I use my time?" and "What should I entrust to systems?" will linger. It is a practical yet philosophical read for anyone aspiring for transformation.
