## 🎯 Core of the Book
This book presents a practical guide based on the three principles of investment: "long-term, diversified, and low-cost." Authors 山崎元 and 水瀬ケンイチ outline a "minimal yet powerful strategy" that enables individual investors to advance their asset formation based on their own judgment, free from the distractions of market noise and the sales tactics of financial institutions.

Their proposed "ほったらかし投資術" (hands-off investment strategy) is not merely about neglecting investments; rather, it is based on a rational philosophy that avoids wasting time on areas that cannot be improved through effort. The uniqueness of this book lies in positioning investment not as the center of life, but as a mechanism to expand happiness and freedom.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Index investing is a reproducible success method**  
  Index investing, which tracks market averages, allows anyone to achieve the same results without relying on personal ability or timing. Given that many active management strategies underperform the market average, it is recommended as a method that combines certainty and fairness.

- **"Holding on" is the essence of investing**  
  Rather than focusing on short-term trading or market predictions, the attitude of staying invested in the market leads to long-term success. The "power of continuity," which involves not stopping contributions even during market downturns, is key to maximizing the effects of compounding.

- **Cost reduction is the only certain return**  
  Differences in trust fees and sales commissions can lead to significant disparities in long-term outcomes. The authors assert that "fees are risk-free negative returns," emphasizing the importance of choosing low-cost funds as a top priority.

- **Breaking free from dependence on financial institutions**  
  The book encourages becoming a "self-reliant investor" who is not swayed by the profit structures of sales companies. Paying for advice should be replaced by self-education and rational decision-making, which leads to true freedom.

- **The purpose of investing is to expand freedom and happiness**  
  The goal is to achieve "freedom from the company" through financial independence, positioning investment as a part of life. The ultimate aim is not financial success, but the realization of spiritual abundance.

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
The expansion of the new NISA system, which began in 2024, supports individual investors in long-term, diversified, and tax-exempt operations, aligning perfectly with the principles outlined in this book. Particularly, the "permanence of the accumulation investment framework" is leading to a societal shift towards "hands-off" investment behaviors rather than short-term trading.

Furthermore, as inflation and rising interest rates progress, index investing is being reevaluated as a means of asset protection compared to savings. Low-cost funds like "ｅＭＡＸＩＳ Ｓｌｉｍ 全世界株式" proposed by the authors are precisely optimized choices for the modern investment environment.

Thus, this book serves as a "modern textbook" for individuals seeking to independently build their assets in an era where institutional changes and the maturation of investment culture intersect.

## 🚀 Practical Suggestions
1. **Consolidate and regularly invest in a global stock index fund**  
   By regularly investing in "ｅＭＡＸＩＳ Ｓｌｉｍ 全世界株式（オール・カントリー）," you can automatically diversify your investments across global stock markets. This eliminates complex decision-making and maximizes the power of continuity.

2. **Design your risk tolerance using the "one-third rule"**  
   Determine your investment amount based on whether you can tolerate losing one-third of it after one year. This helps prevent emotional turmoil and allows you to continue investing calmly even during downturns.

3. **Incorporate individual government bonds (variable 10 years) as a safe asset**  
   These maintain stable value even in rising interest rate environments and are safer than savings. Balancing them with risk assets increases the overall stability of your portfolio.

4. **Utilize online brokerage to minimize costs**  
   Choose platforms like SBI Securities or Rakuten Securities that offer no-load and low-trust fee products. Cost reduction leads to a guaranteed improvement in returns.

5. **Continue contributions even during market fluctuations**  
   Market declines can present buying opportunities, and continuing contributions can lower your average acquisition cost. Resisting short-term fears and maintaining a long-term perspective is key to success.

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『全面改訂 第3版 ほったらかし投資術』 redefines investment as a rational system for achieving freedom in life, making it a modern classic. By avoiding flashy success stories and market predictions, the authors pursue reproducibility and rationality, providing readers with a deep sense of reassurance.

In the new NISA era, this book holds immense value as the first read for investment beginners. For anyone looking to think about asset formation from a long-term perspective, this book will serve as a compass for "investment without confusion."
