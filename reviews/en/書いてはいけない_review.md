## 🎯 The Core of This Book
『書いてはいけない』 is a "letter of farewell to Japanese society" written by economic analyst Takuro Morinaga, who has been diagnosed with terminal cancer. The author thoroughly exposes the collusion among the media, bureaucracy, politics, and the entertainment industry that conceals the truth.  

Focusing on three "taboos"—the sexual abuse issues at Johnny's Entertainment, the "cult-like" behavior of the Ministry of Finance, and the Japan Airlines Flight 123 crash—he depicts the reality of a Japanese society dominated by fear and deference. Through his own experiences of exclusion, the author embodies how the silence of reporting erodes democracy.  

This book is not merely an exposé; it is also an ethical inquiry that questions the "courage to speak the truth." Against a society that chooses silence, the author argues that "revealing the truth is the first step toward becoming an independent nation."  

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Structure of Control through Fear and Deference**  
  From the power structure of Johnny's Entertainment to the Ministry of Finance's belief in tax increases, the common thread is "control through fear." The author sharply reveals the structure in which media silence is not due to direct pressure but voluntary deference.  

- **Critique of the Ministry of Finance's "Zaimu Cult"**  
  The author portrays the Ministry of Finance's self-serving tax increase as "cult-like," prioritizing organizational interests over the public's well-being. He points out the dangers of managing economic policy through religious beliefs, striking at the essence of bureaucratic control.  

- **The Japan Airlines Flight 123 Incident and the Chain of Sovereignty Loss**  
  By questioning the official explanations, the author interprets the process through which post-war Japan became subordinate to the United States. He positions this incident as the "starting point of Japan's loss of independence," arguing that uncovering the truth is key to national regeneration.  

- **Media Silence and the Crisis of Democracy**  
  The author clarifies the structural weaknesses of media outlets that fear tax audits and advertising pressure, declaring that silence is a complicity with power. He confronts readers with the ethical weight of reporting the truth.  

- **A Life-Risking Record of Truth**  
  As the author states, "I want to complete this book while I am still alive," this work is a crystallization of personal resolve and sense of mission. Even in the face of death, the dignity of a person resisting silence permeates the entire text.

## 🔍 Relevance to Contemporary Context
Since the publication of this book, discussions surrounding the Johnny's issue, distrust in the Ministry of Finance's policies, and the Japan Airlines Flight 123 incident have reignited. Movements to break the "wall of silence" in platforms outside traditional media, such as social media and YouTube, have spread, indicating that Morinaga's insights were ahead of their time.  

Moreover, the ongoing phenomenon of sold-out copies in bookstores as of 2026 reflects a high level of societal distrust and interest in taboos. As media trust wavers, the importance of individuals' ability to choose information—media literacy—has never been more crucial. Morinaga's revelations are not just exposés but are read as resistance to the structures that enforce silence.

## 🚀 Suggestions for Action
1. **Develop a Perspective to See Through Media Silence**  
   Focus on the "unreported aspects" of news and consciously interpret gaps and biases in information as the first step to uncovering structures of control.  

2. **Do Not Take Information from the Ministry of Finance at Face Value**  
   Question the fixed notion that "tax increases = good" and adopt a self-learning attitude toward economic policy. Knowledge-based independence serves as a bulwark against bureaucratic control.  

3. **Support Citizen Movements Demanding Transparency**  
   Take an interest in movements that seek to disclose national incidents and public documents, amplifying voices that advocate for transparency, which contributes to the health of democracy.  

4. **Be Aware of Supporting Independent Reporting**  
   To support reporting free from advertising or tax pressures, individuals can back independent media and journalism funds, recognizing that personal actions can change society.  

5. **Do Not Isolate Individuals Who Break Silence**  
   Cultivating a culture that supports whistleblowers and critical voices is key to preserving a free public space. Society should build a structure that supports courageous speech.  

---

## ⭐ Overall Evaluation
『書いてはいけない』 is a book that combines the courage to confront taboos with a scathing critique of a silent society. The author's writing, imbued with the awareness of his impending death, poses a fundamental question about "what it means to speak the truth as a human being," transcending the corruption of reporting and politics.  

This book should be read not only by those who distrust the media and politics but by anyone who does not want to become a "silent accomplice." It serves as a final warning to modern Japan, urging readers toward resolve and action.
